**Facts from the page:** iPhone only · logs places you stop (≈5 min) into a timeline, no check-ins, no typing ·
names from Apple Maps, travel mode between stops · tags (food, coffee, workout, fun, outdoors) · up to 4 photos, an
emoji and a one-line note per place · weekly 9:16 recap card (places, #1 spot, workouts, first-time spots) for
Stories · 7-day emoji map · imports a Google Maps Timeline export · data on the iPhone, no account, no servers, no
analytics, no feed · uses visits iOS already detects, not continuous GPS · export JSON / GPX, pause, delete all ·
Free to start; Pro $3.99/month or $24.99/year with a 7-day trial (monthly and yearly Wrapped, full history).

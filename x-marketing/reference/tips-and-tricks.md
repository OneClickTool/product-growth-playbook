# Tips and tricks, labelled by how sure we are

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Code** | Follows from X's open-source ranker ([xai-org/x-algorithm](https://github.com/xai-org/x-algorithm)) |
| **Help** | Stated on an official X help page |
| **Practice** | Common practice among builders; not proven, test it on your account |

## Writing
- **Code** · Write for sharing: checklists, tools, surprising numbers. Copy-link share is the top-weighted action (20).
- **Code** · End with a real question. Replies weigh 5; between mutual follows, 15 more.
- **Code** · Don't post five times in an hour. Each extra post from you in a feed is decayed.
- **Code** · A post has 48 hours in For You. Repurpose a good one weeks later with new words, never a copy.
- **Practice** · First line = the whole post if nobody expands it. Put the number or the problem there.
- **Practice** · One idea per post. Two ideas = two posts on two days.
- **Practice** · Native video (screen recording with captions) for demos; GIFs for tiny UI details.
- **Practice** · Lowercase, plain sentences read more human than polished marketing copy. Match your real voice.

## Premium
- **Help** · Reply prioritization: Basic < Premium (larger) < Premium+ (largest). Use it daily under bigger posts.
- **Help** · Longer posts up to 25,000 characters. Put the payoff before the "Show more" cut.
- **Help** · Edit a post within 1 hour (original posts and quotes). Fix typos instead of deleting and losing replies.
- **Help** · Media Studio (Premium and up) to store and reuse demo clips.
- **Help** · Don't change photo, name or @handle often: the checkmark goes away until re-review.
- **Help** · Articles are listed under Premium+ on the help page; check your tier before planning around them.

## Links and selling
- **Code** · Link clicks weigh 0.2. A link is fine; the post just has to earn reach without the click.
- **Practice** · Test link-in-post vs. link-in-first-reply for a month on your own account; keep the winner.
- **Practice** · One ask per seven posts. The pinned post carries the offer the rest of the time.
- **Practice** · UTM on every link (`utm_source=x&utm_medium=bio|post|reply`), or you can't judge Premium's value.

## Things to stop believing (no evidence in the code)
- "Premium makes every post go 10× further." Third-party correlation, not X data.
- "Hashtags get you banned." Not in the code; just low value outside real communities.
- "Post at 9am EST." Your audience's time zone matters more; test.

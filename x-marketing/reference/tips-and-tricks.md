# Tips and tricks, labelled by how sure we are

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Code** | Follows from X's open-source ranker ([xai-org/x-algorithm](https://github.com/xai-org/x-algorithm)) |
| **Help** | Stated on an official X help page |
| **Statement** | Said publicly by X staff or its owner, not in code or help pages |
| **Real** | Seen on the maintainer's own account ([flow/analytics-pattern.md](../flow/analytics-pattern.md)); small base, a direction not a law |
| **Practice** | Common practice among builders; not proven, test it on your account |

## Writing
- **Code** · Write for sharing: checklists, tools, surprising numbers. Copy-link share is the top-weighted action (20).
- **Code** · End with a real question. Replies weigh 5; between mutual follows, 15 more.
- **Code** · Don't post five times in an hour. Each extra post from you in a feed is decayed.
- **Code** · A post has 48 hours in For You. Repurpose a good one weeks later with new words, never a copy.
- **Practice** · First line = the whole post if nobody expands it. Put the number or the problem there.
- **Practice** · One idea per post. Two ideas = two posts on two days.
- **Practice** · Native video (screen recording with captions) for demos; GIFs for tiny UI details.
- **Practice** · Lowercase, plain sentences read more human than polished marketing copy. Match your real voice.

## Premium
- **Help** · Reply prioritization: Basic < Premium (larger) < Premium+ (largest). Use it daily under bigger posts.
- **Help** · Longer posts up to 25,000 characters. Put the payoff before the "Show more" cut.
- **Help** · Edit a post within 1 hour (original posts and quotes). Fix typos instead of deleting and losing replies.
- **Help** · Media Studio (Premium and up) to store and reuse demo clips.
- **Help** · Don't change photo, name or @handle often: the checkmark goes away until re-review.
- **Statement** · Articles (long-form, up to 25,000 characters, own tab on your profile) opened to every Premium
  tier on 2026-01-07, not only Premium+ ([X Creators](https://x.com/XCreators/status/2012306734568849653),
  [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/x-opens-articles-to-all-premium-users-ending-exclusive-pricing-tier/)).
- **Practice** · An Article is seen like any post: in followers' feeds and possibly For You in the first ~48 hours,
  then mostly through your profile's Articles tab, search and links. No public source says how For You ranks
  Articles, and creators have complained of low reach. So always post a short teaser (the payoff + one image) that
  links the Article, pin it for the week, and reuse it in replies where it answers a question.

## Links and selling
- **Code** · Link clicks weigh 0.2. A link is fine; the post just has to earn reach without the click.
- **Statement** · July 2026: X's head of product said links no longer need to go in replies, and Musk said X hasn't
  penalized link posts "for over a year" ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/x-drops-year-old-link-penalty-musk-tells-zuckerberg-on-platform/)).
  No formal announcement.
- **Practice** · So test link-in-post vs. link-in-first-reply for a month on your own account; keep the winner.
- **Practice** · One ask per seven posts. The pinned post carries the offer the rest of the time.
- **Practice** · UTM on every link (`utm_source=x&utm_medium=bio|post|reply`), or you can't judge Premium's value.

## Hashtags
- **Code** · No hashtag signal in the ranker's weights; the model scores a post by predicted actions on its content,
  so it doesn't need tags to know what a post is about ([x-algorithm-and-premium-facts.md](x-algorithm-and-premium-facts.md)).
- **Statement** · Elon Musk asked users to stop using hashtags in late 2024, saying the system doesn't need them
  anymore and they look ugly ([FOX 5 DC](https://www.fox5dc.com/news/hashtags-x-elon-musk-says-please-stop-using-them)).
  The Grok screenshot he shared with it may have been edited, per the same report.
- **Help** · Many unrelated hashtags, or trending hashtags used to hijack a conversation, count as spam under the
  [Authenticity policy](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/authenticity).
- **Practice** · Use one only when a real community follows that tag (an event, a challenge, a niche that searches
  it). Otherwise 0. Plain keywords ("Mac app", "Claude Code") do the job.

## Things to stop believing (no evidence in the code)
- "Premium makes every post go 10× further." Third-party correlation, not X data.
- "Hashtags get you banned." Not in the code; just low value outside real communities.
- "Post at 9am EST." Your audience's time zone matters more; test.

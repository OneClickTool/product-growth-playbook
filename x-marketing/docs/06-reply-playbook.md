# 06 · Reply playbook (the Premium lever)

Agent version with a prompt: [social-x-reply](../skills/social-x-reply/SKILL.md). Target list template:
[templates/reply-targets.md](../templates/reply-targets.md).

## Why replies first
- Premium puts subscribers' replies higher in conversations; the boost grows from Basic to Premium to Premium+
  ([About X Premium](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/x-premium)).
- In the ranker, a predicted reply weighs 5, and 15 more between mutual follows
  ([03-how-x-ranks-posts.md](03-how-x-ranks-posts.md)).
- Under a big post, a good reply is seen by that post's audience, which is usually far bigger than yours.

## The 20-minute session
1. Open your target list (notifications on for 5 accounts).
2. Pick 5–10 posts under ~2 hours old.
3. Reply with one shape: number · counter-example · screenshot · short how-to · honest question.
4. Link to your product only when it answers the post, once, with "I built this".
5. Reply back to anyone who answers you.
6. Log the count in [templates/weekly-numbers.md](../templates/weekly-numbers.md).

## Never
- Automated replies, likes, follows or DMs ([X automation rules](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/x-automation)).
- "Great post!", "This 👆", copy-paste replies across posts.
- Fights. Blocks and reports are the heaviest negative signals.

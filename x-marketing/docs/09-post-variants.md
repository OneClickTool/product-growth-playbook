# 09 · Post variants: 20 kinds of X post and when to use each

The 7-day rotation in [05-post-formats.md](05-post-formats.md) says *which day* gets *which kind* of post. This page
lists every kind (variant) that works for an indie dev selling software, grouped into 5 families. Each variant has a
copy-ready file in [templates/variants/](../templates/variants/README.md) in the
[post-file](../templates/post-file.md) format.

Why so many? Because the ranker's author-diversity decay and "not interested" signal punish sameness
([03-how-x-ranks-posts.md](03-how-x-ranks-posts.md)). Rotating variants keeps every post fresh while the facts behind
them come from one [fact bank](../templates/fact-bank.md).

## The 5 families

| Family | Job | Share of the week | Main signal it earns |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🎬 **Show** | Let people see the product working | 2 of 7 | Follow, profile visit |
| 🧠 **Teach** | Give value that works without buying | 2 of 7 | Copy-link share (weight 20), bookmark |
| 📊 **Prove** | Real numbers and real users | 1 of 7 | Reply, bookmark, trust |
| 💬 **Talk** | Start conversations, meet people | 1–2 of 7 | Reply (weight 5, +15 between mutuals) |
| 💰 **Sell** | Ask, once | max 1 of 7 | Profile visit → landing → trial |

Weights are the defaults in X's open-source ranker; see [03-how-x-ranks-posts.md](03-how-x-ranks-posts.md).

**Paid, later:** a *Promoted post* is not a separate variant. When one organic post clearly beats the rest (replies,
profile visits, trials), you can promote that exact post from its activity view; X logs each promotion as its own
campaign and says there's no minimum campaign spend
([How to increase your reach](https://help.twitter.com/en/managing-your-account/increase-x-reach),
[X Ads campaigns 101](https://business.x.com/en/help/campaign-setup/campaigns-101)). Ads with "bonus / claim your
offer" creative are a different game: only after the funnel converts organically
([strategy-spend-and-timing](../../skills/strategy/strategy-spend-and-timing/SKILL.md)).

## A week built from variants

| Day | Family | Pick one |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | 🎬 Show | V01 demo · V02 before/after · V04 tour |
| Tue | 📊 Prove | V10 number · V12 user feedback |
| Wed | 🧠 Teach | V05 tip · V06 checklist · V08 comparison · V09 free resource |
| Thu | 💬 Talk | V14 behind the scenes · V15 failure · V17 contrarian |
| Fri | 💬 Talk | V16 question / poll · V13 community intro (new community) |
| Sat | 🧠 Teach | V07 how-to long post |
| Sun | 📊 Prove + 💰 Sell | V11 weekly numbers, with V19 offer in the reply (or V20 in a launch week) |
| Any day | 🎬 Show | V03 shipped today, when you ship (counts as that day's post) |
| Daily | 💬 Talk | V18 quote posts and plain replies: [06-reply-playbook.md](06-reply-playbook.md) |

Use each variant at most once a week, and don't repeat the same variant for the same product two weeks in a row.

## A real comparison (two posts, same account, same hour)

> **Real case, the maintainer's account, screenshot 2026-10-12.** Small base and only minutes after posting, so read
> it as a direction, not a result.

| Post | Variant | Early numbers | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Hi Build in Public 👋 I'm Leo, solo dev shipping small Mac + Windows apps. All my specs, plans and Claude skills live in .md files…" | V13 community intro | ~142 views, 9 replies, 10 likes at ~24 min | Spoke to one community, in first person, with a real problem; the community surfaced it to members |
| "Ever wondered how to make your landing page stand out, convert visitors… Stop reinventing the wheel. Start building smarter." + a landing screenshot | none (generic hook) | 4 views at ~13 min | Ad-style opener, two clichés, and the image (a Mac cleaner landing) didn't match the text (Claude skills) |

Rewrite of the second post as a **V02 before / after** (fill the `<…>` with real facts):

```text
Rebuilt the landing page of my Mac cleaner with one Claude skill file.

Before: feature list, no demo.
After: one hook, a 30-second demo, one "Try free" button.

<what changed in clicks, or "will share numbers in 2 weeks">
```

Image: the old page next to the new one. Link to the skill or the page in the first reply.

## Rules every variant follows
Short version below; the full page is [10-best-practices.md](10-best-practices.md).

1. The first line works alone in the feed: a problem, a number or a result. No "Ever wondered…", "Here's the thing…".
2. The image or clip shows exactly what the text says.
3. One idea per post. The link goes in the first reply or the bio, unless you're testing link-in-post.
4. Every number is real, dated and has its base ([social-credible-posting](../../skills/social/social-credible-posting/SKILL.md)).
5. Answer every reply in the first hour.

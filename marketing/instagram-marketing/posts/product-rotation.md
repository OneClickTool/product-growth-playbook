## Sweepy · Hero

**Facts:** duplicates found byte for byte, even across folders · keeps the plain-named first copy · flags
.dmg/.pkg older than 7 days · flags files not opened in 90+ days and files of 500 MB+ (never pre-selected) · real
thumbnails · sorts loose files by type and month (`Images/2026-09`) · skips live downloads · moves to Trash, never
deletes · undo 7 days (Free) / 30 days (Pro) · Free watches 2 folders, Pro unlimited · macOS 14+, Mac App Store.

**Carousel A · I05 mistakes, 8 slides**
| # | Text | Image |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `5 things quietly filling your Mac` | Downloads with `report (1).pdf, report (2).pdf…` |
| 2 | `None of them is "your photos".` | Storage bar (demo Mac) |
| 3 | `1 · Copies: file (1), file (2), file (3)` | Duplicate list |
| 4 | `2 · Installers you used once (.dmg, .pkg)` | Old .dmg rows |
| 5 | `3 · Files you haven't opened in 90+ days` | Flagged rows, unticked |
| 6 | `4 · A few 500 MB+ files you forgot` | Big-file rows |
| 7 | `5 · Loose files with no folder` | Sorted `Images/2026-09` folders |
| 8 | `Send this to someone whose Mac is full. Sweepy finds all 5. Free for 2 folders → link in bio` | App icon + handle |

**Carousel B · I02 before/after, 4 slides:** `Downloads: before` (messy) → `One scan` (result list) → `After` (sorted
by type and month) → `Moved to Trash, not deleted. Undo for 7 days. → link in bio`.

**Reel · I07, 15 s:** 0–1 s messy Downloads · 1–6 s scan, duplicates + old `.dmg` marked · 6–10 s clean · 10–13 s
Undo, files back · 13–15 s end card `Free for 2 folders`. Post as a Trial Reel first.

**Caption line 1:** `How to find duplicate files on a Mac and clean your Downloads folder`
**Hashtags:** `#macapps #macos #macbook #productivitytips`

## Haunts · Hero 2 (in testing: "building", never "download now")

**Facts:** iPhone only · logs places you stop (≈5 min) into a timeline, no check-ins, no typing · names from Apple
Maps, travel mode between stops · tags (food, coffee, workout, fun, outdoors) · up to 4 photos, an emoji and a
one-line note per place · weekly 9:16 recap card (places, #1 spot, workouts, first-time spots) for Stories · 7-day
emoji map · imports a Google Maps Timeline export · data on the iPhone, no account, no servers, no analytics, no feed
· uses visits iOS already detects, not continuous GPS · export JSON / GPX, pause, delete all.

**Single · I10:** the weekly recap card itself (demo route only, no real home address) on a 3:4 canvas with
`My week, as a card.` above it. Caption: what's on the card + `Building this for iPhone. Launch email → link in bio`.

**Carousel · I08 behind the scenes, 5 slides:** `A location app I'd actually install` → `No account` → `No servers,
no analytics, no feed` → `Uses the visits iOS already detects, not GPS all day` → `Export JSON / GPX or delete
everything. Building it now → launch email in bio`.

**Story · I11:** frame 1 the recap card · frame 2 poll `Would you share a weekly places recap?` Yes / Only to friends ·
frame 3 `Get one email at launch` + sticker `Join the waitlist` (`utm_medium=story&utm_campaign=haunts`).

**Caption line 1:** `An iPhone app that remembers the places you went, without check-ins`
**Hashtags:** `#iphoneapps #iosapps #travelapp #journaling`

## Edge Tabs · Reach

**Facts:** paste lines → a clean checklist (bullets, numbers, blank lines removed) · up to 10 notes as colored tabs
on the right edge · slide out on hover · never steals focus · in every Space, even full screen · progress bar per
note · toss with Undo · history 30 days · ⌘N, ⌘1–⌘9, Space to tick · offline, no account · macOS 14+ · free.

**Reel · I07, 12 s:** copy 5 lines from a chat → paste → checklist appears as a colored tab on the screen edge → hover
→ Space to tick → tab tucks away → end card `Free. No account.`
**Carousel · I04, 6 slides:** `Turn any list into a checklist on your Mac` · one step per slide · last slide
`Free, no ads → link in bio`.

**Haunts** (the weekly recap card). Edge Tabs and Markdown Everywhere ride the niche account; AgentBell gets Reels
only; Redliner gets "building" posts to grow a waitlist.

## Week 3 · Oct 26–Nov 1 · Borrow audiences
- Brand: 7 posts, only the top 2 formats + 1 new. The week's **one offer post**: Sweepy "Free for 2 folders, here's
  what Pro adds".
- Hook lab: 3 ideas × 3 hooks; winners shared to followers.
- Niche account 2 for Haunts' audience (places, journaling, travel memories): 5 posts, **waitlist** link, "building".

## Week 4 · Nov 2–8 · Multiply winners
- Brand + niche: 7 + 7 + 7 posts; every week-3 winner gets its Reel version, music-slideshow cut and X post.
- Hook lab: 4 ideas × 3 hooks.
- Creators: 80 DMs; renew the 20 % with landing visits; their best Reel → **partnership ad**.
- Collab: 5-maker roundup carousel (all as collaborators) + one creator collab.
- **Gate:** landing visits and trials by `utm_medium` (bio / story / dm / creator) and by `utm_content`.

## Nov 9–10 · Decide
- Keep the 2 engines with the lowest cost per trial / signup, cut the weakest one, keep the rest flat.
- Hooks that won twice → into the brand's hook bank. Formats that lost 4 times → dropped.
- Next sprint: double the winning engine's inputs (more creators, or more niche posts, or more hook tests).

## Daily (60–90 min)
Publish on every account · answer every comment and DM in the first hour · Story with link sticker → Highlight ·
10 real comments in the niche · 10–20 creator DMs · check yesterday's Trial Reels.

## Stop signs (act the same day)
- Account Status shows a recommendation limit on any account → pause that account's posting, read
  [08](../docs/08-rules-and-risks.md), fix, appeal if wrong.
- A niche account starts reposting others' content to keep up → stop; fewer original posts beat any reposts.
- Ad cost per trial > 2× target for 3 days → kill the ad, keep the post.
- Out of real facts for a product → skip it; never fill slides with claims you can't back.

# Instagram facts, with sources (checked 2026-10-12, messaging 2026-10-13)

Labels: **Official** = Instagram / Meta page or Mosseri's own post. **Reported** = press coverage of an Instagram
announcement (the original post wasn't reachable when checked). **Practice** = common advice, not confirmed by
Instagram; test it. Features roll out unevenly by country and account: confirm in your own app before you rely on one.

## Ranking

| Fact | Label | Source |
|---|---|---|
| There is no single algorithm: Feed, Stories, Explore and Reels each have their own ranking | Official | [Instagram Ranking Explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained) (May 31, 2023) |
| Feed predicts: time spent, comment, like, share, tap on the profile photo. Feed avoids many posts from one person in a row | Official | same |
| Explore weights post popularity more than Feed; it predicts likes, saves and shares; most of it is from accounts you don't follow | Official | same |
| Reels predicts: reshare, watch to the end, like, visit the audio page. Surveys ask viewers if a Reel was worth their time | Official | same |
| Reels with low resolution, watermarks, muted audio, borders, **mostly text**, or already posted on Instagram are shown less | Official | same |
| Watermarked Reels get less distribution to non-followers | Official | same |
| Posts that break the Recommendation Guidelines stay up but are less likely to be recommended (Explore, Reels, Search, Suggested). Repeat breaks make the whole account ineligible for a while; check **Account Status** | Official | same |
| Top three signals: **watch time, likes, sends**. Read them per reach (likes per reach, sends per reach), not as totals | Reported | [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/) (Jan 2025) |
| Likes matter slightly more for people who follow you, sends slightly more for people who don't | Reported | same |
| Accounts that mostly repost others' work are no longer recommended. Extended from Reels to photos and carousels; judged over about a month of posts; crediting the creator is not enough | Reported | [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/instagram-restricts-reach-of-content-aggregators-in-new-crackdown/) (Apr 30, 2026) |
| "Sends are worth 3–5× likes" and similar multipliers | Unverified | Only in marketing blogs. Don't quote it |

## Formats and sizes

| Fact | Label | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Photos and carousels accept **3:4** (1080 × 1440). The profile grid is a taller rectangle since Jan 2025 | Reported | [Lindsey Gamble](https://www.lindseygamble.com/blog/instagram-now-supports-34-aspect-ratio-for-photos-and-carousel-posts) (May 2025) · [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/instagram-head-announces-big-changes-3-minute-reels-and-new-look-for-profile-grid-461527-2025-01-21) (Jan 2025) |
| Carousels hold **up to 20** slides; the first slide's ratio is used for all slides | Reported | [Decktopus guide](https://www.decktopus.com/blog/instagram-carousel-size-dimensions) and others; confirm in the app |
| Reels can be **up to 3 minutes** (was 90 s) | Reported | [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/instagram-head-announces-big-changes-3-minute-reels-and-new-look-for-profile-grid-461527-2025-01-21) (Jan 2025) |
| **Trial Reels** go to non-followers first; ~24 h later you see views, likes, comments, shares and can share with followers (or let Instagram do it if it does well in 72 h) | Reported | [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/social-media/instagram-will-let-video-creators-make-trial-reels-for-non-followers-160016725.html) (Dec 2024) |
| Design at 1080 px wide; Instagram stores photos at up to 1080 px wide | Practice | widely repeated; matches what the app exports |

## Growth levers (checked 2026-10-12)

| Fact | Label | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Posting frequency tracks follower growth with diminishing returns: weekly growth ≈ +0.12 % (1–2 posts/week), +0.26 % (3–5), +0.44 % (6–9), +0.66 % (10+); a week without posting lowers growth. Feed posts only, correlational, ~2 M posts / 100k accounts | Reported (study) | [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/) (Aug 2025) |
| **Trial Reels** opened to creators with a **public** account and **≥ 1,000 followers** | Reported | [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-expands-access-trial-reels/752895/) |
| **Collab posts** appear on every collaborator's profile and in all their followers' feeds; engagement is pooled. Cap reported as 5 collaborators + author (older guides say 3) | Reported | [Sked Social](https://skedsocial.com/blog/instagram-collaboration-feature.md); confirm in the app |
| Music can be added to **photo carousels** (≥ 2 photos, not when the carousel has video) | Reported | [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2023/08/14/instagram-now-lets-users-add-music-to-photo-carousels) (Aug 2023) |
| **Broadcast channels**: one-to-many messages to followers (text, photos, video, polls), global since Jun 2023 | Official | [Meta newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news//2023/02/instagram-broadcast-channels-creators-deepen-connections-with-followers/) |
| **Partnership ads** (brand runs ads from a creator's post): Meta data relayed by press, on average **19 % lower CPA** and **13 % higher CTR** than standard brand ads | Reported | [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/meta-expands-partnership-ads-turn-creator-content-performance) |
| **Engagement custom audiences** include people who engaged with your content on Instagram; website custom audiences retarget site visitors (pixel); no custom audiences for under-18s | Official | [About engagement custom audiences](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1090330204367211/?id=2469097953376494) · [Website custom audiences](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/610516375684216) |
| Comment → DM flows: vendor benchmarks put link clicks around **18–25 %** of people who enter the flow; one benchmark shows the link in message 2 (after a button) converting better than in message 1 | Practice (vendor data) | [Inrō glossary](https://www.inro.social/glossary/dm-click-through-rate) |
| Messaging rules: the person starts the conversation; the standard window for messages is **24 h**; the **human agent** tag (up to 7 days) is for a person answering manually | Official (checked 2026-10-13) | [Messenger Platform and IG Messaging API policy](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/messenger-platform/policy) · [Send messages](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/instagram-api-with-instagram-login/messaging-api) |
| **Private replies**: one message in reply to a comment on your post, Reel or ad, sent within **7 days** of the comment; it links to the comment | Official (checked 2026-10-13) | same |
| Several accounts are fine (the app switches between up to 5); using accounts to **deceive people about who runs them** or to evade enforcement is banned | Official | [Switch accounts](https://help.instagram.com/1696686240613595/) · [Inauthentic Behavior policy](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/inauthentic-behavior/) |
| Paid / gifted creators must disclose the material connection clearly; fake reviews and fake endorsers count as endorsements under the 2023 FTC Endorsement Guides | Official (US) | [Manatt summary of the 2023 Guides](https://www.manatt.com/insights/newsletters/advertising-law/an-in-depth-look-at-the-ftcs-updates-to-the-endor) |
| Cal AI (calorie app): growth driven by **250+ paid fitness creators** posting product demos, upfront multi-video deals, then influencer clips reused as Meta / TikTok ads; reported 20–30k downloads a day | Reported (founder interviews) | [Latka](https://getlatka.com/interviews/cal-ai-zach-yadegari-2025) · [Superframeworks](https://superframeworks.beehiiv.com/p/50m-exit-at-19-cal-ai) |

## What this kit does NOT claim
- Best posting times by hour, "the algorithm hates links in bio", "post 3× a day", engagement-pod tricks: none of
  these come from Instagram. Where the kit suggests a time or a number, it is labelled **Practice**.

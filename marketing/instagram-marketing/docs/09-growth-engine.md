# 09 · The growth engine: Operator mode

Docs 01–08 are **Starter mode**: one account, 5 posts a week, Stories, a link path. That builds a base. It does not
move hundreds of products. Teams that scale apps on Instagram run **several engines at once**, push volume until
returns flatten, test hooks on strangers before followers see them, borrow other people's audiences (creators,
collabs), and pour paid money **only on posts that already won organically**. This page is that system.

```text
            ┌────────────── 1 CONTENT FACTORY (1 fact → 10 assets) ──────────────┐
            ▼                         ▼                         ▼                  ▼
   2 HOOK LAB (Trial Reels)   3 OWNED NETWORK (2–4 niche   4 CREATOR ENGINE    5 COLLAB SWAPS
      winners → followers        accounts, disclosed)       (paid + gifted)     (shared posts)
            └──────────────┬──────────┴──────────────┬───────────┴────────────────────┘
                           ▼                         ▼
                 6 DM ENGINE (comment → DM → link)   8 SEARCH (Instagram + Google)
                           ▼
                 7 PAID AMPLIFIER (boost winners, partnership ads, retarget engagers)
                           ▼
                 landing (utm_source=instagram) → trial / email → paid
```

Hard line, because it is also the growth line: **no bought followers, no bots, no fake reviews, no accounts that
pretend to be independent users.** They get the account limited (Account Status) or banned, and the FTC treats fake
endorsers as endorsements ([08](08-rules-and-risks.md), [facts](../reference/instagram-facts.md#growth-levers-checked-2026-10-12)).
Everything below is aggressive *and* clean.

## Engine 1 · Content factory: 1 fact → 10 assets (batch day, 4–6 h/week)

Never make one post from one fact. Make a **pack**:

| # | Asset | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Carousel 8–10 slides (the [image flow](04-image-flow.md)) | Brand feed | The master asset |
| 2 | Same slides, 5-slide cut **with trending music** (photo carousel + audio) | Niche account | Music on photo carousels is supported (Reported) |
| 3–5 | **3 Reels, same body, 3 different hooks** (first 1–2 s) | Trial Reels | The hook lab, engine 2 |
| 6 | Reel cover / single result image | Brand feed or niche account | Grid + Explore |
| 7 | 3-frame Story with link sticker | Brand Stories → Highlight | The visit |
| 8 | Broadcast channel message ("new: …" + poll) | Brand channel | Followers who opted in |
| 9 | X post / long post | X kit | Same fact, new cut |
| 10 | TikTok photo-mode slideshow + the best Reel | TikTok | Same files, no watermark crossing over; process: [TikTok slideshow kit](../../tiktok-marketing/README-TikTok-Marketing.md) |

Tools (any): Canva bulk create or Figma components for slides; CapCut or Instagram's Edits app for Reels; code
(HTML/CSS or Remotion) if you render slides from data. One batch day yields **5–7 packs = 50–70 assets**.

## Engine 2 · Hook lab (Trial Reels)

Reach is decided in the first second. Professionals don't guess hooks, they test them on strangers.

1. Every Reel goes out as **3 Trial Reels** with the same body and 3 hooks (pain · number · before/after).
   Trial Reels need a public account with ≥ 1,000 followers (Reported). Below that, run the test on TikTok or on a
   niche account (engine 3) and post only the winner to the brand.
2. At +24 h compare **views and shares** (Trial Reel insights). Winner = highest shares per view; ties → views.
3. Winner: **share to followers**, pin it if it beats your top 3, and send it to engine 7 for paid.
4. Losers stay hidden. Log the hook text and result in [templates/hook-tests.md](../templates/hook-tests.md).
5. After 30 tests you have a **hook bank for your niche** that beats any generic list. Start from
   [11-hook-bank.md](11-hook-bank.md).

Volume target: **10–15 Trial Reels a week** (3–5 ideas × 3 hooks).

## Engine 3 · Owned niche network (2–4 accounts)

One brand account can only be about you. Buyers follow **topics**: Mac tips, iPhone tricks, productivity setups,
desk/workflow, travel journaling. Run 2–4 accounts you own, each a useful page on one topic:

- **80 % pure value** (tips, settings, shortcuts, setups) made by you; **20 %** shows one of your products where it
  genuinely fits.
- **Disclose in every bio:** `Mac tips by the makers of Tidybox & Pinlog` (fictional). Owning several accounts is allowed;
  hiding who runs them to look like independent fans is the inauthentic behaviour Meta bans (Official).
- **Original only.** No reposting other people's tips or Reels: since Apr 2026, accounts that mostly repost aren't
  recommended at all (Reported). Same fact, your own screenshots, your own words.
- Cadence: **1 post a day each** (carousel or music slideshow), made in the content factory. Different visuals per
  account; never the same file on two accounts on the same day.
- Each niche account links to the brand account and to a landing page with `utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=bio&utm_campaign=<account>`.
- Kill rule: an account with no growth in followers and no landing visits after 6 weeks is paused.

Why it works: Buffer's data shows growth rising with posting volume up to 10+ posts a week (correlational,
Reported). Spread that volume across topic accounts instead of spamming one feed.

## Engine 4 · Creator engine (the biggest lever)

Cal AI's reported growth came from **250+ paid fitness creators** making product-demo videos, signed on multi-video
upfront deals, with their clips reused as ads (Reported, founder interviews). You can run a small version of that
for any product with a visible result. Full program, DM scripts, brief and pricing: [10-creator-program.md](10-creator-program.md).

| Tier | Creators | Deal | Weekly work |
|---|---|---|---|
| $0 | 10 nano (1–10k) a month | Free Pro / lifetime license + affiliate code, no obligation to post positively | 30 DMs |
| $500/mo | 5–8 micro (5–50k) | Paid pack of 3 Reels each + usage rights for ads | 60 DMs, 2 briefs |
| $2k+/mo | 15–30 micro on retainer | Monthly pack + bonus per 100k views + partnership-ads rights | 100+ DMs, a part-time manager |

Rules: Paid partnership label on every paid post; creators keep their own voice and honest opinion; one UTM / promo
code per creator; double down on the 20 % whose posts bring landing visits, drop the rest.

## Engine 5 · Collab swaps (borrow audiences for free)

A **Collab post** shows on every collaborator's profile and in all their followers' feeds, and likes / comments are
pooled (Reported; up to 5 collaborators per guides). Every week:

- 1 collab with a **complementary indie maker** (a Mac app that isn't a competitor): a joint carousel "5 Mac apps
  that fix X", each slide one app, all makers as collaborators.
- 1 collab with a **creator** from engine 4 (their Reel, you as collaborator).
- Monthly: a "best small apps of the month" roundup with 4–5 makers as collaborators.

## Engine 6 · DM engine (the click machine)

Caption links aren't clickable; DMs are. On 1–2 posts a week:

```text
Last slide + caption:  "Comment MAC and I'll send you the full list + the app"
Message 1 (auto):      "Here you go 👋 Want the checklist and the free app link?"  [button: Send it]
Message 2 (on tap):    the checklist (real) + landing URL with utm_medium=dm&utm_content=<post-id>
```

Vendor benchmarks: ~18–25 % of people entering the flow click; the link in message 2, after a button, beat
message 1 (Practice, vendor data). Use a Meta-partner tool on the official API; the DM must give exactly what was
promised. Opt-ins also build a list you can message later (inside Meta's messaging rules).

**Broadcast channel:** open one on the brand account; post launches, betas, discount windows. It's a free push to
people who asked for it (Official).

## Engine 7 · Paid amplifier (money only on proven winners)

| Step | Do | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Pixel + Conversions API | On every landing page; events: view, trial/signup, purchase | Before spending anything |
| Boost winners | The post / Reel that won engine 2 or beat your 30-day average on sends per reach | $10–20/day, 3–5 days |
| Partnership ads | Run ads from creators' posts (with their permission) | Meta-reported −19 % CPA vs brand ads |
| Retarget | Engagement custom audience: Instagram engagers 30 days + site visitors 30 days, not converted (Official) | Show the offer carousel / testimonial |
| Lookalike | From trial starters or purchasers | Only after ~100+ conversions |
| Kill | No trial/signup after spend = 2× your target CPA | Stop the ad, keep the post |

Paid never rescues a post that lost organically. It multiplies a winner.

## Engine 8 · Search (Instagram + Google)

Public posts from professional accounts can rank on Google (Reported, Jul 2025). Treat every caption line 1, alt
text and slide 1 as a search result for one query: `how to find duplicate files on mac`, `best mac menu bar apps`,
`iphone app to remember places`. Keep a list of 30 queries per product (from Google autocomplete and your store
keywords) and give every carousel one query. Evergreen carousels keep sending visits for months.

## The operator week (≈ 12–15 h + daily 60–90 min)

| Day | Brand | Niche accounts (each) | Hook lab | Creators / collabs | Paid |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Carousel (teach) + Story link | 1 post | 3 Trial Reels (idea A) | 20 creator DMs | Review last week's ads |
| Tue | Reel winner from last week + Story | 1 post | 3 Trial Reels (idea B) | Brief 1–2 creators | Boost last week's winner |
| Wed | Carousel (send-able) + **DM keyword** | 1 post | Pick A winner → followers | Collab post with a maker | — |
| Thu | Single result / behind the scenes + Story link | 1 post | 3 Trial Reels (idea C) | 20 DMs | Retarget refresh |
| Fri | Carousel (offer, max 1/week) + broadcast | 1 post | Pick B winner | Creator collab goes live | Partnership ad from best creator post |
| Sat | Music slideshow (light) + Story "what shipped" | 1 post | Pick C winner | 20 DMs | — |
| Sun | **Batch day** (engine 1) + numbers | queue the week | log tests | pay creators, send briefs | budget for next week |

Daily 60–90 min: publish, answer every comment and DM in the first hour on every account, Stories, 10 real comments
on accounts in your niche, creator DMs.

## The operator dashboard (Sunday, 20 min)

| Engine | Number to watch | Good sign | Act when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Factory | Assets shipped / week | ≥ 40 | < 25 two weeks running → simplify formats |
| Hook lab | Winner share rate vs your median | Rising | No winner in 2 weeks → change the idea, not the hook |
| Network | Non-follower % and landing visits per account | Rising | Flat 6 weeks → pause the account |
| Creators | Landing visits and trials per creator (UTM / code) | Top 20 % pay back | Bottom 50 % → don't renew |
| Collabs | New followers in 48 h after each collab | Above a normal day | Two flat collabs with the same type → change partner type |
| DM | Clicks ÷ flow entries | ≥ 15 % | < 10 % → fix the opener / button |
| Paid | Cost per trial vs target | ≤ target | > 2× target → kill |
| Search | Google clicks to the profile / posts (Search Console on your site for landing pages) | Rising | — |

Funnel line (same as [07](07-metrics-and-stop-rules.md)): impressions → profile → link tap → landing → trial →
paid. Every Sunday, one engine gets more time and the weakest one gets cut.

## Ramp, so you don't burn out in week 2

| Weeks | Turn on |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Starter mode + engine 1 (factory) + engine 6 (DM) + engine 8 (search captions) |
| 3–4 | + engine 3 (one niche account) + engine 5 (one collab/week) + first 10 creator DMs |
| 5–8 | + engine 2 (hook lab, once ≥ 1,000 followers) + engine 4 paid pack + engine 7 boosts |
| 9+ | Scale the two engines with the best cost per trial; cut the rest |

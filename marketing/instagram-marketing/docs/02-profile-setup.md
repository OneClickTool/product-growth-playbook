# 02 · Profile setup checklist (45 min, once)

The profile is the page every post sends people to. If it doesn't say what you make and give one obvious link,
reach never turns into visits.

## Account
- [ ] **Professional account** (Creator or Business): you get Insights, and only professional accounts' public posts
      can be shown in Google / Bing ([facts](../reference/instagram-facts.md#links-and-search)). Check the setting
      "Allow public photos and videos to appear in search engine results" is on.
- [ ] Account Status shows **no recommendation limits** (Settings → Account Status). Fix anything listed first.

## Name, handle, bio
- [ ] **Name field** = searchable words, not only your brand: `Sam · Mac & iPhone apps` beats `SamWorks`.
      (Practice: the name field is matched in Instagram search.)
- [ ] **Bio, 3 lines:** who you help · what you make · the offer.
      `Small Mac & iPhone apps that tidy your digital life.`
      `Built solo, no accounts, no tracking.`
      `↓ free apps + 14-day trials`
- [ ] Use `#` or `@` in the bio only if you want that tap (they become links, [Official](../reference/instagram-facts.md#links-and-search)).

## Links (up to 5, Reported; check Edit profile → Links)
Order matters: the first link shows on the profile, the others behind "and N more".

| # | Title (short) | URL |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The hero product or a page listing all products | `…?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=bio&utm_campaign=<slug>` |
| 2 | Email list / launch waitlist | `…?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=bio&utm_campaign=list` |
| 3–5 | One per product you're pushing this month | same pattern, `utm_campaign=<slug>` |

Update link 1 when the post of the day talks about another product, so "link in bio" always lands right.

## Highlights (Story archives on the profile)
- [ ] 3–5 Highlights, one per product + one "Start here". Each holds the product's best Story frames **with the link
      sticker**, so the link outlives the 24 hours ([06](06-stories-and-links.md)).
- [ ] Covers in your design kit colors with one word: `Tidybox`, `Pinlog`, `Free`, `Start`.

## Pinned posts (up to 3)
1. The best carousel of your hero product (the one with the most sends).
2. A 20–30 s Reel demo.
3. "Who I am + what I build" single image or carousel.

## Profile photo
Your face or your hero app icon on a solid design-kit color. Same picture as on X and GitHub so people recognise you.

## Done when
- [ ] A stranger can say in 5 seconds what you make and where to tap.
- [ ] Every link carries UTM and opens the right page on a phone.

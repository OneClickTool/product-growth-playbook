# 08 · Rules and risks

What cuts your reach or the account, and how to stay clear. Sources: [reference/instagram-facts.md](../reference/instagram-facts.md).

## Reach killers (Official unless marked)

| Risk | What happens | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| **Other apps' watermarks** on Reels | Less distribution to non-followers | Export the original file, or record again |
| **Mostly-text Reels**, borders, muted audio, low resolution | Shown less | Text → carousel; Reels show the app moving, with sound |
| **Reposting** a Reel already on Instagram | Shown less | Make a new cut: new hook, new order, new cover |
| **Account of mostly other people's work** (Reported, Apr 2026) | Not recommended at all, photos and carousels too | Your own screenshots, numbers, words. Credit isn't enough |
| Breaking the Recommendation Guidelines repeatedly | The whole account stops being recommended for a while | Check **Account Status** monthly; appeal if wrong |
| New account or Community Guidelines strikes | No link sticker | Build a clean history first; use bio links |

## Account killers (Practice: patterns Instagram enforces against inauthentic behaviour)
- Buying followers, likes or views; engagement pods; follow/unfollow tools.
- Third-party apps that log in with your password to automate likes, follows or comments.
- Mass identical DMs or comments. One-to-one replies by hand are fine; keyword → DM through an official
  Meta-partner tool is fine when the DM gives exactly what was promised.

## Honesty rules (from this repo's [CLAUDE.md](../../../CLAUDE.md))
- No invented numbers, users, reviews or results on slides. A number needs a date and, if small, the base
  (`212 trials`). Made-up examples carry the **Illustrative example** label in this kit, and never go on a real post.
- No fake screenshots: AI images never imitate your app's UI or a result it didn't produce.
- Unreleased products: say "building" / "coming to iPhone", never "download now".
- Paid or gifted posts from others about your product: they must use Instagram's **Paid partnership** label and
  follow the FTC / local ad rules.

## Bait that backfires
- "Like if you agree", "Tag 3 friends", "Comment YES" with nothing behind it: likes go up, sends and saves don't, and
  the account looks spammy.
- "Comment X for the link" on every post. Use it on one post a week at most.
- Misleading hooks (the slide promises something the post doesn't deliver): people stop swiping, and swiping is
  the signal.

## Music
Business accounts have a smaller music library. Use Instagram's royalty-free library, your own voice, or music you
licensed for commercial use. Never upload a popular song you don't have rights to on a product Reel.

## Privacy in screenshots
Use a demo user, demo files and a demo location. Blur or remove: real names, emails, home / work addresses,
personal file paths, notifications, account balances.

# 01 · Strategy: from zero followers to the first paid subscriber

The whole X flow in one page. Every other doc zooms into one part of it.

## The chain

```text
post / reply  →  profile visit  →  link click  →  landing  →  trial / install  →  paid
  (daily)         (profile)          (bio, pin)     (site)       (store)            (paywall)
```

You can only fix the weakest link. Measure each one weekly ([07-metrics-and-stop-rules.md](07-metrics-and-stop-rules.md))
before posting more.

## Five decisions to make once

| Decision | Default for an indie dev selling software | Doc / skill |
|---|---|---|
| Who you talk to | The people who'd pay, not other indie devs only. Builders are good for reach, buyers pay | [05-post-formats.md](05-post-formats.md) |
| Hero product | The one with a paid plan, a trial and a 10-second "aha" | [social-x-profile-funnel](../skills/social-x-profile-funnel/SKILL.md) |
| The offer | Exactly what the store says: "Free, Pro $9.99 once", "7-day trial, then $3.99/mo" | [monetization-free-trial-vs-freemium](../../../skills/monetization/monetization-free-trial-vs-freemium/SKILL.md) |
| Time budget | 30 min/week batching + 20 min/day replies | [flow/daily-flow.md](../flow/daily-flow.md) |
| Stop rule | 60 days with growing profile visits and zero trials → fix offer/landing, not X | [04-premium-30-day-plan.md](04-premium-30-day-plan.md) |

## Why this works with a small account
- X's ranker gives authors with few impressions a **new-author boost**, so small accounts get tested
  ([03-how-x-ranks-posts.md](03-how-x-ranks-posts.md)).
- Replies are the cheapest reach. Premium's reply prioritization puts yours higher ([06-reply-playbook.md](06-reply-playbook.md)).
- A post lives 48 hours in For You. Daily posting keeps you visible without repeating yourself.
- You already have content: everything you shipped. The fact bank turns it into posts
  ([templates/fact-bank.md](../templates/fact-bank.md)).

## What "fast first dollar" realistically means
Nobody can promise a date. What you control: a working trial on the hero product, a pinned demo, and 30 days of
daily posts and replies with UTM links. After 30 days you'll know which link in the chain is weak. Don't buy
followers or engagement; it breaks X rules and the FTC treats fake engagement as deceptive
([08-rules-and-risks.md](08-rules-and-risks.md)).

# 10 · X best practices, on one page

![A maker with a checklist next to a big glowing Post button](../../../docs/images/illustrations/08-best-practices.jpg)

Everything the kit says about posting, in the order you use it. Each rule carries its evidence label from
[reference/tips-and-tricks.md](../reference/tips-and-tricks.md): **Code** (X's open-source ranker), **Help** (an
official X help page), **Real** (the maintainer's own account, small base), **Practice** (common among builders, test
it yourself).

## 1 · Before you write
- **Practice** · Write from the [fact bank](../templates/fact-bank.md), never from "what sounds good". No fact, no post.
- **Practice** · Pick the variant first ([09-post-variants.md](09-post-variants.md)), then the product. One idea per post.
- **Code** · Rotate variants and products. Repeating yourself triggers author-diversity decay and "not interested"
  (−47.5), which costs far more than a like earns (0.5) ([03-how-x-ranks-posts.md](03-how-x-ranks-posts.md)).

## 2 · The first line (the hook)
The first line is the whole post for most people scrolling. It must work alone.

| Hook type | Pattern | Fits variants |
|---|---|---|
| Problem in their words | "Your Downloads folder has 14 copies of the same PDF." | V01, V19 |
| Number with base | "Trial → paid went from 3% to 9% in 3 weeks. 212 trials." | V10, V11 |
| Result + method | "Got through App Review first try, after 2 rejections: one notes file." | V07, V15 |
| Decision | "Decided my app stays one-time purchase." | V14 |
| Direct question | "Mac folks: sync to iPhone, or Mac only?" | V16 |
| Community address | "Hi Build in Public 👋 I'm …" | V13 |

- **Real** · Two posts, same hour (2026-10-12): a first-person intro to a Community got ~142 views and 9 replies in
  ~24 min; an ad-style "Ever wondered how to…" post got 4 views in ~13 min. Details in
  [09-post-variants.md](09-post-variants.md#a-real-comparison-two-posts-same-account-same-hour).
- **Practice** · Don't open with: "Ever wondered…", "Here's the thing…", "Stop reinventing the wheel", "Game-changer",
  "Unlock", "Supercharge", "In today's fast-paced world", "🧵👇", "👀". Readers skip what sounds like an ad or AI.

## 3 · The body
- **Practice** · Short lines, blank line between ideas, specific nouns (file names, minutes, prices).
- **Practice** · Plain first-person voice. Lowercase and imperfect beats polished marketing copy.
- **Practice** · At most one emoji. Hashtags: 0, or 1 that a real community follows (**Code**: no hashtag weight in the
  ranker; **Help**: many unrelated or hijacked trending tags count as spam,
  [Authenticity policy](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/authenticity)).
- **Code** · End with a real question when you want replies (weight 5, +15 between mutual follows).
- **Practice** · Every claim true, dated, with its base. No fake proof
  ([social-credible-posting](../../../skills/social/social-credible-posting/SKILL.md)).

## 4 · Media
- **Practice** · The image or clip shows exactly what the text says. A mismatched image (text about A, picture of B)
  makes people scroll past.
- **Practice** · Demos: native screen recording, 10–30 s, starts on the problem, captions burned in (most people watch
  muted). GIF only for tiny UI details.
- **Practice** · Screenshots: crop to the part that matters, readable on a phone, personal data removed.
- **Help** · Premium tiers get longer posts (up to 25,000 characters), longer video, edit within 1 hour and Media Studio
  ([reference/x-algorithm-and-premium-facts.md](../reference/x-algorithm-and-premium-facts.md)).

## 5 · Links
- **Code** · Link clicks weigh 0.2. The post must earn reach without the click.
- **Practice** · Default: link in the first reply (`link: first-comment`) or the bio. Test link-in-post for a month
  if you want; keep what brings more landing visits ([tips](../reference/tips-and-tricks.md#links-and-selling)).
- **Practice** · UTM on every link: `utm_source=x&utm_medium=bio|pinned|reply|post&utm_campaign=<product>`.
- **Real** · The same URL in many posts and replies, during a sudden activity spike, came right before a temporary
  reach label ([08-rules-and-risks.md](08-rules-and-risks.md)). Vary, and keep links few.

## 6 · Volume and timing
- **Code** · A post lives about 48 hours in For You; extra posts from you in one feed are decayed. 1–3 strong posts a
  day, spaced hours apart, beat a burst.
- **Real** · Going from ~1 to ~15 actions a day in 3 days took impressions ~10× and also drew a temporary label.
  Ramp up over 2–3 weeks ([flow/analytics-pattern.md](../flow/analytics-pattern.md)).
- **Practice** · Post when *your buyers* are awake; check X analytics after 2 weeks instead of trusting "post at 9am".
  Starting point when you live in Asia and sell to US users: post around **19:00–23:00 Vietnam time (ICT, UTC+7)**,
  which is US East Coast morning (08:00–12:00 EDT; one hour earlier in winter).

## 7 · The first hour after posting
- **Code** · Replies are weighted; a live conversation keeps the post going inside its 48 hours.
- **Practice** · Stay for the first hour. Answer every reply with something more than "thanks!".

## 8 · Replies and quote posts (the daily lever)
- **Help** · Premium's documented reach feature is reply prioritization ([About X Premium](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/x-premium)).
- **Real** · On the maintainer's account the days with the most replies were the days with the most reach.
- **Practice** · 5–10 replies a day to accounts your buyers follow, each adding a number, example or screenshot. Quote
  posts (V18) only when you add something. Playbook: [06-reply-playbook.md](06-reply-playbook.md).
- **Practice** · Under ~1,000 followers, split your time **70% replies, 30% own posts**. Follow 20–30 niche accounts
  with notifications on, reply within ~15 minutes, and ramp up to 20–30 replies a day over 3–4 weeks
  ([06-reply-playbook.md](06-reply-playbook.md#under-1000-followers-70-replies-30-posts)).

## 9 · X Communities
- **Help** · Community posts are visible beyond members: in members' Home timelines, to your followers, in search and
  on your profile. Anyone can view; only members can take part, and moderators can limit non-member replies. You
  need a public account to join ([Communities on X](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/communities)).
- **Practice** · Join 2–3 Communities where your buyers or fellow builders are. Read the rules, introduce yourself
  (V13), then post there only what fits the room: questions, behind the scenes, free resources. No offers unless
  the rules allow them.

## 10 · Profile and safety
- **Practice** · The profile converts visits into clicks: name line with buyer words, bio with the free offer, UTM
  link, pinned demo ([02-profile-setup.md](02-profile-setup.md)). Fix it before posting more.
- **Help** · Changing photo, display name or @handle removes the checkmark until re-review. Banner, bio and pinned post
  are safe to change.
- **Help** · No duplicate posts, no automated replies or follows, no scripted website use
  ([X automation rules](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/x-automation)).

## 11 · Measure by variant
- **Practice** · Every post file carries `variant:` ([templates/variants](../templates/variants/README.md)). Each
  Sunday, list the week's posts with profile visits and link clicks, and note which variant they were.
- **Practice** · After 4 weeks keep the 2 variants with the most profile visits per post, drop the weakest, and try one
  you haven't used ([07-metrics-and-stop-rules.md](07-metrics-and-stop-rules.md)).

## 12 · Paid, only after organic works
- **Help** · You can promote an existing post from its activity view; each promotion is its own campaign, and X says
  there's no minimum campaign spend ([How to increase your reach](https://help.twitter.com/en/managing-your-account/increase-x-reach),
  [X Ads campaigns 101](https://business.x.com/en/help/campaign-setup/campaigns-101)).
- **Practice** · Promote the organic post that already brings profile visits and trials, not a new ad. Set a stop
  budget first ([strategy-spend-and-timing](../../../skills/strategy/strategy-spend-and-timing/SKILL.md)).

## Before you press Post

```text
[ ] Variant picked; not used for this product last week
[ ] First line works alone: problem, number, result, decision or question
[ ] None of the banned openers or AI words
[ ] One idea; ≤ 1 emoji; 0–1 hashtag
[ ] Image / clip shows exactly what the text says, readable on a phone
[ ] Every number real, dated, with its base
[ ] Link in first reply or bio, with UTM (or no link)
[ ] I'll be here for the first hour to answer replies
```

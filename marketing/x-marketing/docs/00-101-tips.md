# 00 · X 101: 100 tips you can use today

> **New here? Read this first.** One line per tip, each labelled by how sure we are. The long reads are the
> [docs](../README-X-Marketing.md#docs-in-reading-order); this page is the short layer. Format and rollout:
> [marketing 101 plan](../../README-marketing-101-plan.md). Facts checked **2026-10-21**; X changes often.

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Code** | Follows from X's open-source ranker ([xai-org/x-algorithm](https://github.com/xai-org/x-algorithm)) |
| **Help** | Stated on an official X help page |
| **Statement** | Said publicly by X staff or its owner, not in code or help pages |
| **Real** | Seen on the maintainer's own account ([flow/analytics-pattern.md](../flow/analytics-pattern.md)); small base, a direction not a law |
| **Practice** | Common practice among builders; not proven, test it on your account |

Ranker weights quoted below are the defaults in the open-source code; production may run other values
([x-algorithm-and-premium-facts.md](../reference/x-algorithm-and-premium-facts.md)).

## 1 · Profile & account

1. **Practice** · Use a clear face photo for a solo maker, or a sharp logo for a studio — people follow people more
   readily than a blurry mark. [02-profile-setup.md](02-profile-setup.md)
2. **Practice** · Write the bio as *who you help → what they get → how* — a visitor decides in seconds whether to
   follow. [02-profile-setup.md](02-profile-setup.md)
3. **Practice** · Use the banner as a billboard: the product, or the one topic you post about — it's the biggest
   thing a profile visitor sees.
4. **Practice** · Put buyer words in the display name ("Sam | Mac apps for messy desktops") — the name line shows on
   every reply you write.
5. **Practice** · Keep the @handle short and easy to type; avoid random numbers and underscores — people retype it
   from memory.
6. **Practice** · Pin your best demo or offer post — the pinned post sells while you're not posting.
   [social-x-profile-funnel](../skills/social-x-profile-funnel/SKILL.md)
7. **Practice** · Swap the pinned post when the campaign changes, not every day — a stale pin sells last month's
   offer.
8. **Practice** · One link in the bio, with UTM (`utm_medium=bio`) — one clear next step, and you can measure it.
   [resources/README.md](../resources/README.md)
9. **Practice** · Put one real, dated proof point in the bio ("2 Mac apps live since 2025") — specific beats
   "passionate builder". Never an invented number ([fake proof](../reference/fake-proof-and-scams.md)).
10. **Help** · Don't change your photo, display name or @handle while on Premium — the checkmark goes away until X
    reviews the account again ([About X Premium](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/x-premium)). Banner, bio and pin are
    safe.
11. **Help** · Don't use stock, stolen or AI-generated photos or a copied bio to look like someone else — that's a
    "fake persona" under the [Authenticity policy](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/authenticity).
12. **Help** · Run a separate account per product only if each has its own purpose — X allows up to 10 accounts for
    non-duplicative purposes, and bans cross-posting the same content between them
    ([Authenticity](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/authenticity)).
13. **Practice** · Keep one voice, one colour and 3–5 topics — people recognise you in the feed before reading the
    name.
14. **Practice** · Fix the profile before posting more — more posts into a weak profile just leak visits.
    [02-profile-setup.md](02-profile-setup.md)

## 2 · Posting

15. **Practice** · Pick 3–5 content pillars and stay inside them — the ranker learns who likes you from what you
    post. [05-post-formats.md](05-post-formats.md)
16. **Practice** · Six posts give, one asks — the pinned post carries the offer the rest of the time.
    [01-strategy-first-dollar.md](01-strategy-first-dollar.md)
17. **Practice** · Write from the [fact bank](../templates/fact-bank.md), never from "what sounds good" — no fact, no
    post.
18. **Practice** · Pick the [variant](09-post-variants.md) first, then the product — it stops you writing the same
    post twice.
19. **Practice** · The first line is the whole post for most people; put the problem, number, result or question
    there. [10-best-practices.md](10-best-practices.md#2--the-first-line-the-hook)
20. **Practice** · Don't open with "Ever wondered…", "Game-changer", "Unlock", "🧵👇" — readers skip what sounds like
    an ad or AI.
21. **Real** · A first-person intro beat an ad-style post in the same hour (~142 views vs 4) — write like a person.
    [09-post-variants.md](09-post-variants.md#a-real-comparison-two-posts-same-account-same-hour)
22. **Practice** · One idea per post; two ideas = two posts on two days.
23. **Practice** · One sentence per line, blank line between ideas — most people read on a phone.
24. **Practice** · Specific nouns beat adjectives: "14 copies of the same PDF", not "lots of clutter".
25. **Practice** · Cut 30% after the first draft — every extra word lowers the chance of a full read.
26. **Practice** · If AI drafts it, rewrite it in your words — readers and repliers spot AI tone fast.
27. **Practice** · At most one emoji, as a marker, not decoration.
28. **Practice** · Build in public: numbers, mistakes, decisions — a dated story earns trust a feature list can't.
    [social-build-in-public](../../../skills/social/social-build-in-public/SKILL.md)
29. **Practice** · Share a failure with what you changed — it gets replies, and replies are weighted.
    [v15-failure-story](../templates/variants/v15-failure-story.md)
30. **Practice** · Post a checklist, template or prompt people want to keep — saving and sharing posts is the point.
    [v06-checklist](../templates/variants/v06-checklist.md)
31. **Practice** · Before/after screenshots show a result in one glance.
    [v02-before-after](../templates/variants/v02-before-after.md)
32. **Practice** · A contrarian take, said politely and with a reason, stops the scroll; a rude one draws mutes.
    [v17-contrarian-take](../templates/variants/v17-contrarian-take.md)
33. **Practice** · Weekly numbers post ("week 6: 212 trials, 9 paid") — true, dated, with its base.
    [v11-weekly-numbers](../templates/variants/v11-weekly-numbers.md)
34. **Code** · Repurpose a good post weeks later with new words, never a copy — a post has 48 hours in For You, and
    duplicates break the [automation rules](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/x-automation).
35. **Practice** · Write a week of posts in one sitting on Sunday, then schedule them — daily writing is where people
    quit. [social-x-growth](../skills/social-x-growth/SKILL.md)
36. **Practice** · Long posts and threads: one point per part, the payoff before the "Show more" cut, a summary + one
    ask at the end. [v07-how-to-long-post](../templates/variants/v07-how-to-long-post.md)
37. **Help** · A post can carry up to 4 media items (e.g. 2 photos, 1 GIF, 1 video) — use them when they show what
    the text says ([How to Post](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/how-to-post)).
38. **Practice** · The image or clip must show exactly what the text says — a mismatched picture makes people scroll
    past.

## 3 · Reach

39. **Code** · Write for sharing: copy-link share is the top-weighted action (20) — checklists, tools and surprising
    numbers get shared.
40. **Code** · End with a real question when you want replies — replies weigh 5, plus 15 between mutual follows.
41. **Code** · Likes barely count (0.5); stop judging a post by likes. Watch replies, shares and profile visits.
42. **Code** · Don't post five times in an hour — each extra post from you in one feed is decayed.
43. **Code** · 1–3 strong posts a day, hours apart — a post lives about 48 hours in For You.
44. **Code** · Mutes (−58.8), "not interested" (−47.5) and reports (−234) cost far more than anything earns — never
    post what annoys strangers. [03-how-x-ranks-posts.md](03-how-x-ranks-posts.md)
45. **Code** · New accounts: out-of-network posts below an engagement threshold are filtered — early on, replies to
    others are how strangers find you.
46. **Code** · Rotate variants and products — the same post shape every day invites "not interested".
47. **Real** · Ramp activity over 2–3 weeks; jumping from ~1 to ~15 actions a day in 3 days drew a temporary reach
    label. [08-rules-and-risks.md](08-rules-and-risks.md)
48. **Practice** · Post when your buyers are awake. Selling to the US from Vietnam: start with 19:00–23:00 ICT
    (US East morning), then check analytics after 2 weeks.
49. **Practice** · Native screen recordings, 10–30 s, start on the problem, captions burned in — most people watch
    muted.
50. **Help** · Communities posts reach beyond members (members' Home, your followers, search, your profile) — join
    2–3 where your buyers are ([Communities on X](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/communities)).

## 4 · Replies, quotes & DMs

51. **Help** · Premium's documented reach feature is reply prioritization (Basic < Premium < Premium+) — use it
    every day under bigger posts ([About X Premium](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/x-premium)).
52. **Practice** · Under ~1,000 followers, spend 70% of your time on replies, 30% on your own posts.
    [06-reply-playbook.md](06-reply-playbook.md#under-1000-followers-70-replies-30-posts)
53. **Practice** · Reply within ~15 minutes of a big account posting — early replies sit at the top.
54. **Practice** · Every reply adds a number, an example or a screenshot — "Great post!" earns nothing.
    [social-x-reply](../skills/social-x-reply/SKILL.md)
55. **Help** · Keep a private [List](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/x-lists) of 20–50 accounts your buyers follow and
    open it each reply session — a List timeline shows only those accounts.
56. **Practice** · Turn on notifications for 5–10 of those accounts so you can reply early.
57. **Real** · On the maintainer's account, the days with the most replies were the days with the most reach.
58. **Practice** · Stay for the first hour after posting and answer every reply with more than "thanks!".
59. **Practice** · Quote a post only when you add something (a test, a number, a counterexample); otherwise reply.
    [v18-quote-with-value](../templates/variants/v18-quote-with-value.md)
60. **Practice** · At most one reply a day with a link, and only when it answers the question.
61. **Practice** · Answer "what are you building?" posts with one line + one demo; check the drop rules first.
    `/social-x drop`
62. **Practice** · Make friends at your own size — peers reply back; giants rarely do.
63. **Practice** · Tag people who helped you, with what they did — they often share it.
64. **Help** · Host or join a Space for deeper conversations — Spaces are public, anyone can listen, up to 13 people
    can speak, and you can schedule one ([About X Spaces](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/spaces)).
65. **Practice** · DM to start a real conversation, never to pitch on first contact — cold pitches get blocked
    (−31.2).
66. **Practice** · Co-write a thread or swap a "tool I use" post with a peer — each of you reaches the other's
    audience.

## 5 · Links & selling

67. **Code** · Link clicks weigh 0.2 — a link is fine, but the post must earn reach without the click.
68. **Statement** · July 2026: X's head of product said links no longer need to go in replies, and Musk said link
    posts haven't been penalized "for over a year"
    ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/x-drops-year-old-link-penalty-musk-tells-zuckerberg-on-platform/)). No formal
    announcement.
69. **Practice** · So test link-in-post vs link-in-first-reply for a month; keep the one that brings more landing
    visits.
70. **Practice** · UTM on every link (`utm_source=x&utm_medium=bio|pinned|reply|post&utm_campaign=<product>`), or you
    can't tell whether X pays. [utm convention](../../../skills/analytics/analytics-tracking-plan/references/utm-convention.md)
71. **Real** · The same URL in many posts and replies, during an activity spike, came right before a temporary reach
    label — vary links and keep them few. [08-rules-and-risks.md](08-rules-and-risks.md)
72. **Practice** · Link to your landing page, not the store — stores drop UTM, your page keeps it.
    [resources/README.md](../resources/README.md)
73. **Practice** · Send followers to an email list with a free resource — you don't own followers, you own the list.
    [v09-free-resource](../templates/variants/v09-free-resource.md)
74. **Practice** · Post real user feedback with permission — social proof sells better than your own claims.
    [v12-user-feedback](../templates/variants/v12-user-feedback.md)
75. **Practice** · Launch posts tell the story: why you built it, what it fixes, what it costs.
    [v20-launch](../templates/variants/v20-launch.md)
76. **Practice** · Judge X by profile visits, link clicks and trials, not likes.
    [07-metrics-and-stop-rules.md](07-metrics-and-stop-rules.md)
77. **Practice** · A price in a post must match the store today — re-check before you post it.
78. **Practice** · Make the ask once a week (e.g. Sunday), with a reason to act now that is true.
    [v19-offer](../templates/variants/v19-offer.md)

## 6 · Paid features (Premium, ads)

79. **Help** · Premium tiers add longer posts, longer video, edit within 1 hour and bookmark folders; Premium adds
    Media Studio ([About X Premium](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/x-premium)).
80. **Help** · Edit a post within the hour to fix a typo instead of deleting it and losing its replies.
81. **Statement** · Articles (long-form, own tab on your profile) opened to every Premium tier on 2026-01-07
    ([X Creators](https://x.com/XCreators/status/2012306734568849653)) — check your own composer.
82. **Practice** · Post a short teaser (payoff + one image) for every Article and pin it for the week — no public
    source says how For You ranks Articles.
83. **Practice** · Take Premium for 30 days with a goal and UTM links, then decide on numbers.
    [04-premium-30-day-plan.md](04-premium-30-day-plan.md)
84. **Help** · Promote an existing post from its activity view; X says there's no minimum campaign spend
    ([X Ads campaigns 101](https://business.x.com/en/help/campaign-setup/campaigns-101)).
85. **Practice** · Only pay to push a post that already brings profile visits and trials organically, with a stop
    budget set first. [strategy-spend-and-timing](../../../skills/strategy/strategy-spend-and-timing/SKILL.md)
86. **Practice** · Reply usefully under promoted posts in your niche — the ad pays to reach many people, who may see
    your reply. Unverified: test two weeks and compare impressions; never spam ads.

## 7 · Rules & bans

87. **Help** · Never buy followers or use apps that promise them — "using or promoting third-party services or apps
    that claim to add followers" is prohibited ([About following](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/x-follow-limit)).
88. **Help** · No follow churn (following then unfollowing many accounts to grow) — it can get the account suspended
    ([About following](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/x-follow-limit)).
89. **Help** · Follows are capped at 400 a day, with ratio limits after 5,000 follows — "unable to follow" means
    slow down ([About following](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/x-follow-limit)).
90. **Help** · No automated replies, follows, duplicate posts or scripted website use
    ([X automation rules](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/x-automation)). Scheduling your own posts is fine.
91. **Help** · Many unrelated hashtags, or trending tags used to hijack a conversation, count as spam
    ([Authenticity](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/authenticity)).
92. **Help** · Don't use several accounts to like, reply to or boost your own posts — that's coordinated inauthentic
    activity ([Authenticity](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/authenticity)).
93. **Code** · Check the Under the Hood tool for labels that limit your reach before blaming the ranker
    ([x-algorithm](https://github.com/xai-org/x-algorithm)).
94. **Practice** · Never post a revenue screenshot you can't back up, and don't trust others' — fakes are common.
    [fake-proof-and-scams.md](../reference/fake-proof-and-scams.md)

## 8 · Stop believing

95. **Code** · "Hashtags help the ranker find you." There's no hashtag weight; the model scores predicted actions on
    the content. Use 0, or 1 a real community follows.
96. **Statement** · Musk asked users to stop using hashtags in late 2024
    ([FOX 5 DC](https://www.fox5dc.com/news/hashtags-x-elon-musk-says-please-stop-using-them)) — but "hashtags get
    you banned" is false; only spammy use breaks the rules.
97. **Practice** · "Premium makes every post go 10× further." Third-party correlation, not X data; the documented
    feature is reply prioritization.
98. **Practice** · "Post at 9am EST." Your buyers' time zone matters more; test it.
99. **Practice** · "Five posts a day beats one." Author-diversity decay says the opposite (**Code**); one good post
    beats five.
100. **Practice** · "It works in a week." Plan on 3–6 months of daily posting and replies; read the numbers weekly
     and change one thing at a time. [07-metrics-and-stop-rules.md](07-metrics-and-stop-rules.md)

## Keep it current
- A tip a test proves or disproves gets its label changed (Practice → Real, or moved to *Stop believing*), with the
  date.
- Re-check the Help links monthly; X rewrites its help pages without notice.

# A day on X: promote 3–5 products, spend the rest earning trust

The hour-by-hour version of [daily-flow.md](daily-flow.md), for one common setup: you sell **3–5 products** and
want everything else you do on X to bring **impressions, followers and trust**, so that when a product post does go
out, people already know you.

Filled-in version for real products (posts, hooks, links, demo shot lists):
[posts/product-rotation.md](../posts/product-rotation.md) · non-promo posts ready to copy:
[posts/talk-bank.md](../posts/talk-bank.md) · a full week mixing both: [posts/week-02-mix.md](../posts/week-02-mix.md).

Labels: **Code** = X's open-source ranker, **Help** = X help page, **Practice** = common practice, test it
([docs/00-101-tips.md](../docs/00-101-tips.md)).

## The mix (the one rule this page is built on)

```text
Per week                          Promo                     Trust / reach ("talk")
Original posts   ~14 (2 a day)    4–5 (one per product)     9–10  (tips, takes, questions, build log)
Replies          ~50–70           ≤ 1 in 10 has a link      the rest: a fact, number, screenshot or question
Direct ask       1 (Sunday)       "link in reply"           —
```

- **One product per day at most**, and each of your 3–5 products once a week. That's the whole promo budget.
- A product post still has to *give* something (a demo that shows a fix, a lesson, a number). Only Sunday asks.
- The talk posts are not filler: they decide who follows you. Talk about the problems your buyers have, not
  about growth or X itself. If you sell Mac utilities, your talk is Mac tips, AI coding, small-app building.
- Why it works: **Code** · the ranker scores each post by predicted replies, shares and dwell, and mutes / "not
  interested" cost far more than a like earns ([docs/03-how-x-ranks-posts.md](../docs/03-how-x-ranks-posts.md)).
  An account that sells every day trains people to scroll past or mute.

## Pick your 3–5 (once, 15 min)

| Slot | Pick the product that… | Post angle that week |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | has a paid plan or trial and a 10-second "aha" | Demo (Mon) + Sunday offer |
| Reach | is free and solves a loud problem in your niche | Demo or lesson; drives follows |
| Story | you're still building (waitlist) | Behind the scenes, poll on a feature |
| 4th–5th | rotate weekly, or skip | Lesson / tip that leads to it |

Write each one's row in [templates/product-rotation.md](../templates/product-rotation.md): one-line problem, the
offer **as the store says it**, the demo clip, 3 hooks, the UTM link.

## The day, hour by hour

Times are your audience's morning, lunch and evening (**Practice** · test, there's no universal best hour).

### 07:30 · Warm up (5 min)
First: write down **profile visits, last 7 days** from X analytics ([the one number](analytics-pattern.md#the-one-number-to-watch-every-day)).

| Do | Tool |
|---|---|
| Open notifications: answer every reply from last night first | [X notifications](https://x.com/notifications) |
| Open your private reply-targets list, note 3–5 posts under 2 h old | [X Lists](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/x-lists) · or [X Pro](https://pro.x.com) columns (Premium+) |
| Check today's two posts are scheduled | X composer → *Unsent posts → Scheduled* |

### 08:00 · Post 1 of the day (2 min)
- Mon–Sat: the **product slot** of the day *or* the strongest talk post. Sunday: weekly numbers + one offer.
- Product post: link in the **first reply** or in the post; X said in July 2026 it no longer penalizes links
  ([tips](../docs/00-101-tips.md#5--links--selling)). **Practice** · test both for a month. **Code** · a link
  click weighs 0.2, far less than a reply or share, so the post must earn reach without it.
- Stay 15 minutes after posting if you can, so you answer early replies fast (**Practice**).

### 08:05 · Reply session 1 (20 min) — the biggest lever
- 5–7 replies under fresh posts from target accounts ([docs/06-reply-playbook.md](../docs/06-reply-playbook.md)).
- **Help** · Premium ranks subscribers' replies higher; Premium+ the highest
  ([About X Premium](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/x-premium)).
- Every reply adds one thing: a number, a counter-example, a screenshot, a 2-step how-to, or an honest question.
- Product link only when the post literally asks for a tool like yours, or a "what are you building?" post that
  passes the [6 checks](../docs/06-reply-playbook.md#what-are-you-building-posts-drop-your-link-or-not). Say "I built this". Max once a day.
- No hashtags unless a real community uses one ([why](../docs/00-101-tips.md#8--stop-believing)).
- Agent help to draft (you post by hand): [social-x-reply](../skills/social-x-reply/SKILL.md).

### 12:30 · Lunch check (10 min)
| Do | Why |
|---|---|
| Answer every reply on post 1 | **Code** · replies between mutual follows weigh extra; conversation keeps the post alive |
| 3 more replies, this time to **peers** (accounts your size) | Peers reply back; mutual follows grow ([templates/reply-targets.md](../templates/reply-targets.md)) |
| Like + reply to anyone who quoted or mentioned you | Trust: people see you answer |
| 1 quote post *with your own take* if something in your niche is moving | Reach on a topic people already read today. No auto-posting on trends ([X automation rules](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/x-automation)) |

### 17:30 · Post 2 of the day (2 min)
- Always a **talk post** from [posts/talk-bank.md](../posts/talk-bank.md): tip, opinion, question, build log.
- At least 4 hours after post 1 (**Code** · several posts from one author close together get decayed in a feed).

### 17:35 · Reply session 2 (15 min)
- 5 replies, now to **buyer sources**: reviewers, newsletters, communities where your buyers are.
- Join 1–2 [X Communities](https://help.x.com/en/using-x/communities) in your niche; post there only what fits
  their rules.

### 21:30 · Close the day (5 min)
- Answer the last replies on both posts. Don't leave a question hanging overnight.
- Follow 3–5 people you replied to today *and would reply to again*. No bursts, no follow/unfollow
  ([docs/08-rules-and-risks.md](../docs/08-rules-and-risks.md)).
- One line in the [fact bank](../templates/fact-bank.md): what you shipped, learned or measured today.
- Glance at the day's post in X analytics: impressions, **profile visits**, link clicks. Weekly totals go in
  [templates/weekly-numbers.md](../templates/weekly-numbers.md).

## Short on time? Same mix, smaller

| Budget | Posts | Replies | Skip |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 min | 1 (product day or talk) | 5–7 in one session | Lunch, quote posts |
| 60 min (the day above) | 2 | 13–15 | — |
| 2 h | 2–3 | 20–25 | Never more posts; spend the extra time on replies and on recording next week's demo |

## Ramp up, don't jump

> **Real case.** Going from ~1 to 6 posts + 9 replies a day in 3 days got the maintainer's account a temporary
> reach label (removed ~2 h later). Details: [analytics-pattern.md](analytics-pattern.md).

| Week | Posts / day | Replies / day | Follows / day |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | 5 | ≤ 3 |
| 2 | 1–2 | 8–10 | ≤ 5 |
| 3+ | 2 | 13–15 | ≤ 5 |

Never change photo, name or @handle in a ramp-up week (**Help** · the checkmark goes away until re-review).

## What builds each thing you want

| You want | What moves it | Labels |
|---|---|---|
| **Impressions** | Replies under bigger posts within 2 h; one strong post a day; posts people share (checklists, tools, numbers) | Code + Help |
| **Followers** | A clear niche in the bio; demos and tips in that niche; following back peers you talk to | Practice |
| **Trust** | Real dated numbers (also the bad ones); answering every reply; tips with no link; saying "I built this"; the same voice every day | Practice + [FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking) |
| **Profile visits → clicks** | Bio that says who it's for + offer; pinned demo; one link in bio with UTM | Practice, [social-x-profile-funnel](../skills/social-x-profile-funnel/SKILL.md) |
| **Sales** | The Sunday ask + the pinned post + a landing that matches the offer | [docs/01-strategy-first-dollar.md](../docs/01-strategy-first-dollar.md) |

What kills all of them: buying followers or engagement, auto-replies/likes/follows/DMs, the same text twice,
fights (**Code** · blocks and reports are the heaviest negative signals).

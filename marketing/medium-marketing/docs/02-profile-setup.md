# 02 · Profile setup (20 minutes, once)

On Medium, people who liked an article click your name. The profile has one job: show you're a real maker who
writes useful things, and give one clear next step.

## Checklist

| Item | Do | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Name | Your real name, or "Name · Product" if you write as the maker | A brand name alone (reads like a company account) |
| Photo | Your face or a clean avatar, same as on X and GitHub | The app icon |
| Short bio | Who you are + what you write about + who it helps. E.g. "Indie Mac developer. I write about fixing small macOS annoyances." | "Founder of X 🚀 Check out my app!" |
| About page | 3–5 lines: what you build, why, and one link to your site with UTM | A full sales page |
| Links | Your site with `?utm_source=medium&utm_medium=profile` | Several product links |
| Newsletter / email | Turn on email for new stories so followers get them (email reads also earn more, see [facts](../reference/medium-facts.md#partner-program-getting-paid)) | — |
| Pinned / featured story | Your best tutorial, not your launch post | A product announcement |

Field limits change; if a field is short, cut words, don't cut the "who it helps" part.

## Publications you'll write for
List 3–5 publications in your topic before you write ([06](06-publications-and-boost.md)) and keep them in the
[publication tracker](../templates/publication-tracker.md). Their readers become your followers.

## The link that sells
Medium's job is trust; your site does the selling. Every link points to one page that matches the article: a feature
page, a free tool, a template, or a free trial. Use the same UTM pattern everywhere:
`utm_source=medium&utm_medium=article|profile&utm_campaign=<article-slug>`.

# 08 · Rules and risks

Sources: [reference/medium-facts.md](../reference/medium-facts.md). Medium has the final say on what breaks its
rules, so stay well inside the line.

## What gets accounts suspended or stories hidden

| Risk | What Medium says | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| **Stories that exist to promote your product** | Content posted primarily to drive traffic to an external product is spam; content marketing and signup-only stories get no distribution | Reader-first article, product once, disclosed |
| **Link stuffing** | Link farms aren't distributed | One link to your site at the end; other links only to sources |
| **Self-promo in responses** | Responses whose only point is your link are treated as spam (secondary sources) | Reply to the writer's point; no link unless asked |
| **Unedited AI writing** | Can't be paywalled; undisclosed AI gets Network-only distribution | Write it yourself; disclose AI help near the top |
| **Canonical tricks** | Redirecting canonical URLs away from your own URL or medium.com is SEO manipulation | Canonical only to your own original |
| **Importing what isn't yours** | Only import content you have the right to | Your own posts only |
| **Engagement trading** | Clap-for-clap and follow-for-follow groups fall under spam and site misuse in practice (we found no rule that names them; treat as risky) | Grow through publications and sharing outside Medium |
| **Buying traffic to paywalled stories** | Not recommended in Medium's publication guidelines | Share with Friend Links; let paid ads go to your own site |

## Disclosure
Say "I make {{product}}" in the same sentence as any mention. For affiliate links, say they're affiliate links. In
the US the FTC expects clear disclosure ([social-credible-posting](../../../skills/social/social-credible-posting/SKILL.md)).

## If something goes wrong
- **A story gets no distribution:** read it as a stranger. Is it mostly about your product? Remove the promotion, or
  leave it for your followers and move on. Don't delete and repost the same text.
- **Account restricted or suspended:** read the email, check the [suspension reasons](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/21308869037975-Reasons-for-Suspensions-or-Restrictions)
  page, and appeal once through Medium's help center with a calm explanation. Keep your originals on your own blog so
  nothing is lost.

## The platform risk
Medium changes distribution and pay rules often, and publications come and go. Keep the original of every article on
a site you own, and collect emails on your site, not only Medium followers.

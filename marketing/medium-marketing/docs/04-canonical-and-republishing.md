# 04 · Write once, publish twice: canonical links

If you have your own blog, publish there first and republish on Medium with a **canonical link** pointing to your
blog. Google then treats your blog as the original, and Medium brings the readers. Sources:
[Help: Set a canonical link](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/360033930293-Set-a-canonical-link) ·
[Help: Importing a post](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/214550207-Import-post).

## Two ways

| Way | Steps | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **Import tool** | Medium → Import a story → paste your blog URL → edit → publish. It backdates the story and sets the canonical link for you | Moving 2–3 old posts on day one |
| **Set by hand** | Draft or story → ⋯ → More settings → Advanced settings → "This story was originally published elsewhere" → paste URL → Save → Publish | A new post you wrote on your blog this week |

Check it: open the Medium story, View page source, search `canonical`: the first match must be your blog URL.

## Order and timing (practice to test)
1. Publish on your blog. Make sure Google can index it (Search Console → URL inspection).
2. Wait a few days, so your page is likely indexed first.
3. Republish on Medium with the canonical link. Change the title or first paragraph for Medium readers if you like;
   keep the substance.
4. If a publication accepts it, the canonical link stays; tell the editor it's a republish when you submit.

## Things to know
- **Republished stories can still be Boosted**, but only when you, the original author, post them
  ([facts](../reference/medium-facts.md#distribution-and-boost)).
- **Don't point the canonical anywhere but your own original.** Pointing it at a product page or someone else's site
  is SEO manipulation under Medium's publication rules.
- **Only import what you own.** Not a guest post you sold, not someone else's article.
- **No blog yet?** Then write on Medium first. You can move the post to your site later and set the canonical on your
  site to the Medium URL, or the other way round; pick one original and stick to it.

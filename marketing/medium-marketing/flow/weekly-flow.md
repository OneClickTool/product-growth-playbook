# Weekly Medium flow for an indie dev

Total: ~3–4 hours a week for one article, or ~15 minutes if you only mirror your blog. The why:
[docs/01-strategy.md](../docs/01-strategy.md).

## Once (week 1, ≈1 hour)
- Profile, about page, link with UTM ([02](../docs/02-profile-setup.md)).
- Import 2–3 of your best existing posts with canonical links ([04](../docs/04-canonical-and-republishing.md)).
- Find 3–5 publications and fill the [publication tracker](../templates/publication-tracker.md) ([06](../docs/06-publications-and-boost.md)).

## Every week

| Day | Minutes | Do | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | 20 | Pick one topic from real questions (your Reddit answers, support emails, X replies, search) and one [article type](../docs/05-article-playbook.md) | An [article file](../templates/article-file.md) with title, outline, product-mention test |
| Tue–Wed | 90–150 | Write it (`/social-medium write`), add your own screenshots or code | A finished draft |
| Thu | 20 | Edit: first 3 lines, headings, one product mention at most, UTM link at the end, 5 topics. Submit to one publication or publish | Draft submitted or published |
| Fri | 15 | Share it: an X post with the key point, your newsletter, a Reddit answer where it truly answers the thread (Friend Link if paywalled) | 2–3 outside shares |
| Sun | 10 | [Weekly numbers](../templates/weekly-numbers.md), apply the [stop rules](../docs/07-metrics-and-stop-rules.md) | One change for next week |

## Mirror-only mode (≈15 min per post)
If Medium isn't your main channel: every time you publish on your own blog, wait a few days, import it on Medium,
check the canonical link, add 5 topics, share once. That's it.

## Once a month (≈20 min)
- Check which articles brought site visits and trials; write the next one in the same type.
- Update the publication tracker: drop silent publications, add one active one.
- Re-read [08-rules-and-risks.md](../docs/08-rules-and-risks.md) if any story got no distribution.

## Weekly checklist (copy into your notes)

```text
[ ] topic from a real question
[ ] article useful without my product; product ≤ 1 mention, disclosed
[ ] UTM link at the end (utm_source=medium)
[ ] canonical set if it's on my blog
[ ] 5 topics, submitted to 1 publication or published
[ ] shared outside Medium 2–3 times
[ ] numbers filled on Sunday
```

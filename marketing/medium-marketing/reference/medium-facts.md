# Medium facts, with sources

Checked 2026-10-13. Official = Medium's own help center, policy or blog pages. Secondary = news, third-party guides
or one person's test; treat as a hint and verify before relying on it. Medium's help center blocks some automated
readers, so open the links in a browser.

## Rules and spam

| Fact | Source | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Medium can restrict or suspend content and accounts that break its rules, and has the final say | [Medium Rules](https://policy.medium.com/medium-rules-30e5502c4eb4) · [Help: Medium Rules](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/213477928) | Official |
| The most common reason for suspension is spam and site misuse; examples include posting content **primarily to drive traffic to, or raise the search ranking of, an external site, product or service** | [Help: Reasons for suspensions or restrictions](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/21308869037975-Reasons-for-Suspensions-or-Restrictions) | Official |
| Manipulating SEO or domain authority, including redirecting canonical URLs away from the author's own URL or medium.com, isn't allowed in publications | [Help: Publications best practices and guidelines](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/41530252213655-Medium-Publications-Best-Practices-and-Guidelines) | Official |
| You may only import content you have the right to import | [Help: Importing a post to Medium](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/214550207) | Official |

## AI content

| Fact | Source | Type |
|---|---|---|
| AI-generated writing (disclosed or not) can't be paywalled in the Partner Program; "AI-generated" means mostly written by AI with little or no editing. AI outlining and grammar or fact checkers don't count | [Help: AI content policy](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/22576852947223-Artificial-Intelligence-AI-content-policy) | Official |
| Undisclosed AI writing gets Network-only distribution; disclosed AI writing can get General distribution but not Boost | [Help: AI content policy](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/22576852947223) · [Plagiarism Today](https://www.plagiarismtoday.com/?p=42690) | Official + secondary summary |
| AI images should be captioned as AI | [Plagiarism Today](https://www.plagiarismtoday.com/?p=42690) | Secondary (quotes the policy) |

## Distribution and Boost

| Fact | Source | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Three levels: **Network** (followers of you or the publication), **General** (matched to readers by interest), **Boost** (top tier, chosen by people against the Boost guidelines). Stories that break no rules get at least Network | [Help: Distribution](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/360006362473) (updated 2024-08-14) | Official |
| Not distributed: sponsored content, content marketing, stories whose only purpose is signups or traffic, link farms | [Help: Distribution](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/sections/360003462873-Distribution) | Official |
| Boost is picked by a human curation team, about half from publication editors in the Boost Nomination Program, half found by Medium's own curators | [Help: Boost Nomination Program](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/categories/26572799602583-Boost-Nomination-Program) · [Help: what makes a story great for Boosting](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/26716922635159-Which-stories-are-eligible-to-be-Boosted) | Official |
| Stories older than 6 months can't be nominated | [Help: How do I nominate a story](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/26716107269527-How-do-I-nominate-a-story-for-Boosting) | Official |
| A republished (syndicated) story can be Boosted only when posted by the same writer or publisher who first published it | [Help: Boost eligibility](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/26716922635159-Which-stories-are-eligible-to-be-Boosted) | Official |
| Up to 5 topics per story; topics help discovery inside Medium | [Help: Tags / topics](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/214741038-Tags) | Official |

| Fact | Source | Type |
|---|---|---|
| The import tool backdates the story to the original date and adds a canonical link to the original | [Help: Importing a post](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/214550207-Import-post) | Official |

## Partner Program (getting paid)

| Fact | Source | Type |
|---|---|---|
| You need a bank account **and** tax residence in a supported country; the list includes Vietnam, the US, UK, most of Europe, India, Japan and many more. Payouts go through Stripe | [Help: Partner Program eligibility](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/39121627791639-Medium-Partner-Program-eligibility) · [Help: Set up payouts with Stripe](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/360003928833) | Official |
| Two versions of the eligibility page disagree on whether a paid membership and how many stories are required; check the live page before applying | [Help: Application and enrollment](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/115011694187) · [eligibility](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/39121627791639) | Official (conflicting) |
| Unpaid earnings are forfeited if the account can't receive payouts for 180 days in a row | [Help: Set up payouts with Stripe](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/360003928833) | Official |
| Earnings come from paying members on paywalled stories: reading + listening time (a read counts after 30 s), engagement points (claps, highlights, replies), a Boost bonus and the member read ratio | [Help: Earnings calculation](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/360036691193-Calculating-earnings-in-the-Partner-Program) · [Partner Program overview](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/25267383906711-Medium-Partner-Program-overview) | Official |
| Reads from outside Medium earn more: a bonus for external sources (social, direct links), an extra allocation for search-engine reads, and email reads earn more than feed reads | [Help: Earnings calculation](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/360036691193) | Official |
| A Friend Link lets non-members read one paywalled story (after a free sign-up); you still earn from members who use it | [Help: Friend Links](https://help.medium.com/hc/en-us/articles/360006543813-Friend-Links) | Official |

## Not verified (don't rely on)
- "Medium pays $X per 1,000 views". Third-party figures disagree and Medium doesn't publish a rate.
- Any fixed bio length, number of free stories per month for non-members, or best posting time: check the current
  product, they change.
- Claims that a dofollow link from Medium helps your Google ranking (see the nofollow row above).

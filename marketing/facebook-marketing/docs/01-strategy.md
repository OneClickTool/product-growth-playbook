# 01 · Strategy: is Facebook for your product?

## Why Facebook can still work for small software
- **It's where many non-tech buyers already are.** Meta reported 3.60 billion daily active people across its
  apps for June 2026 ([Meta Q2 2026 results](https://investor.atmeta.com/investor-news/press-release-details/2026/Meta-Reports-Second-Quarter-2026-Results/)).
  Meta no longer reports Facebook on its own, so treat that as "the family is huge", not a Facebook number.
- **Groups gather people around one job or hobby.** A group for wedding planners, Excel users, Mac beginners,
  photographers in one city, parents of toddlers, or a game's players is a room of exactly the people a niche app
  wants, and they ask each other for tools.
- **Local and language groups are often the only English-free channel.** For Vietnamese, Thai, Indonesian,
  Filipino or Spanish-speaking buyers, local Facebook groups are often where software questions get asked
  ([research-where-users-ask](../../../skills/research/research-where-users-ask/SKILL.md) lists Vietnamese examples).
- **A Page alone rarely brings strangers.** Organic Page reach is low for new Pages (practice, not a Meta number;
  see [03](03-how-feed-distribution-works.md)), and Meta has tested limiting links in Page posts
  ([TechCrunch, Dec 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/17/facebook-is-testing-a-link-posting-limit-for-professional-accounts-and-pages)).
  Use the Page as a home, not as the channel.

## Fit test: yes, test, or no

| Signal | Points to **yes** | Points to **no** |
|---|---|---|
| Who buys? | Non-developers: small shops, teachers, parents, hobbyists, local services, older users, players of a game | Developers (go to X, Reddit, GitHub) |
| Are there groups for the job your product does? | Search "<job> group", "<tool> users", "<hobby> <city>" finds active groups (posts this week) | No groups, or only dead ones |
| Do those groups ask for tools? | Threads like "which app do you use for…", "how do I…" appear weekly | Only memes, selling, or news |
| Do the rules allow any self-promotion? | A promo day, a monthly thread, "with admin OK", or helpful mentions allowed | "No links, no promotion, ever" in every group |
| Language | You can write in the group's language | You can't, and nobody on the team can |

- 4–5 yes → Facebook groups can be a main channel. 2–3 → **test** for 30 days as a support channel, answers only.
  0–1 → skip; see the [marketing hub](../../README-marketing-social.md#3--which-channel-first).
- Good fits: template shops, planners, budgeting and family apps, photo and design tools, local-service apps
  (booking, invoices), language and study apps, Mac and Windows utilities for beginners, games with a community.
  Weak fits: dev tools, B2B infrastructure, anything whose buyer is a developer.

## The plan: three phases

| Phase | Weeks | Do | Don't | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 · Set up** | 1 | Real profile, a Page that matches the landing, 10–20 groups found and rules saved, join 5 | Post anything about the product | Group tracker has 5 joined groups with rules |
| **2 · Answer** | 2–4 | 2–3 helpful answers a day; mention the product only when it truly fits and say you made it | Drop links in groups that ban them; post the same text in many groups | Admins and members recognise your name; a few profile visits and clicks |
| **3 · Post and own** | 5+ | One rule-checked post per group per month (story, how-to, feedback request); start your own small group when 20+ people use the product | Turn the group into an ad feed; buy members | You know your 3 best groups and the post format that brings signups |

## Answer first, link last
A group member trusts a neighbour, not an ad. The answer has to help even if they never click: the steps, the free
way, two other tools, then "I made X for this, it does the last step for you" with a UTM link if the rules allow.
The full 5-check test for mentioning your product is in [04-groups-playbook.md](04-groups-playbook.md).

## How Facebook fits the other channels
Facebook groups are reach for non-tech and local buyers; Instagram (same company, same Meta Business Suite) is
reach for the visual side. Reuse the same true facts: an Instagram carousel can become a group how-to post, a
Reddit answer can become a Facebook answer in another language. The full map:
[Marketing & Social](../../README-marketing-social.md).

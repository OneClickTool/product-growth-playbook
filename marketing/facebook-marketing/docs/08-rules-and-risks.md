# 08 · Rules and risks

Quotes from [Meta Community Standards: Spam](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/spam/)
unless noted. Checked 2026-10-13; read the page again before relying on it. On top of Meta's rules, **every group's
own rules** apply, and admins can remove you for breaking them even when Meta wouldn't.

## What counts as spam

| Rule (Meta's words, shortened) | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| Posting, sharing, engaging or creating accounts, Groups or Pages "at very high frequencies", manually or automatically | Don't post in 20 groups in an hour, or comment the same reply everywhere |
| Buying, selling or exchanging likes, shares, views, follows or clicks, including engagement giveaways | No bought followers, no "like-for-like" groups, no paid engagement |
| Requiring people to engage before they see promised content ("Like/share-gating") | Never "comment YES / share to get the link" |
| A link that "promises one type of content but delivers something substantially different"; cloaking, deceptive redirects | The link goes straight to the page the post describes |
| Buying, selling or exchanging accounts, Groups, Pages or admin roles | Don't buy a group or a Page with followers |

Branded content that is clearly identified is an exception in the same policy.

## What Meta shows less
Engagement bait, clickbait, links to pages covered with ads or broken, copied-and-pasted comments, and mostly
reposted content get less distribution
([Reducing the distribution of problematic content](https://transparency.meta.com/enforcement/taking-action/lowering-distribution-of-problematic-content)).

## Messenger
If someone messages your Page, you can reply. Meta's messaging policy is built on the person starting the
conversation and a standard 24-hour window for replies
([Messenger Platform policy](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/messenger-platform/policy);
same rules summarised in the [Instagram kit facts](../../instagram-marketing/reference/instagram-facts.md)). Never send
cold messages to group members to pitch the product.

## Disclosure
- Say you made the product, in the post or answer itself.
- If someone is paid or given something to post about your product on Facebook, Meta expects the branded content
  tool and a "Paid partnership" label (as reported by [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/meta-rolls-out-branded-content-on-reels-388801));
  follow the FTC in the US.
- Never present an AI image as a real customer, a real result or a real photo.

## No scraping
Don't collect group members' names, emails or profiles with tools. Meta says automated collection without
permission is against its terms ([How Meta combats scraping](https://about.fb.com/news/2021/04/how-we-combat-scraping/)),
and it's also how email domains get burned ([email-cold-outreach](../../../skills/email/email-cold-outreach/SKILL.md)).

## What to do when…

| Symptom | Likely cause | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| Post "declined" or stuck in "pending" | Group post approval; new member; a link | Read the rules, ask the admin politely, don't repost the same text |
| Removed or muted in a group | Broke a group rule (promo outside the thread, links) | Thank the admin, ask what's allowed; don't argue, don't rejoin with another account |
| "You can't post right now" / temporary block | Posting or commenting too fast, or many reports | Stop for the day; slow down; write each post fresh |
| Link blocked or warned | Redirect chain, shortener, or a page flagged as low quality | Link the final URL, check it loads fast with no redirects; request a review in the notice |
| Page links limited to a few a month | You're in Meta's link-limit test | Put links in the first comment; check the current status in your Page settings |

# 02 · Page and profile setup

About 30 minutes, once. Facts and sources: [reference/facebook-facts.md](../reference/facebook-facts.md).

People who like your answer in a group tap your name. They land on your **personal profile** first, not your Page.
Both have to say, in five seconds, "a real person who makes a real thing".

## 1. Your personal profile (what group members see)

| Field | What to write | Example (illustrative) |
|---|---|---|
| Profile photo | Your face, clear, not a logo | — |
| Intro / bio | What you make and for whom, in plain words | "I make Clipnest, a clipboard app for Mac beginners. Happy to help with Mac questions." |
| Work | "Founder at <product>" linked to your Page | `Founder at Clipnest` |
| Featured / links | Your landing with UTM | `https://example.com/?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=profile` |
| Public posts | A few public posts about what you build, not only private life | — |

> **Illustrative example.** Fictional product.

Professional mode turns a personal profile into a creator profile whose posts can reach a wider audience
([TechCrunch, Dec 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/17/facebook-is-testing-a-link-posting-limit-for-professional-accounts-and-pages),
quoting Meta). It's optional; it doesn't change how groups see you.

## 2. Your Page (the shop sign)

| Field | What to write |
|---|---|
| Name | The product name, the same as on the store and the landing |
| Category | The closest software / app category |
| Bio / intro | Who it's for + what it does + price model ("free, Pro $9 once") |
| Profile picture / cover | App icon / one real screenshot of the result, not a stock photo |
| Website / action button | Landing with `utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=page` |
| Pinned post | A 30-second demo or the before → after, with the link in the post or first comment |
| Messaging | On, with a short auto-reply and an FAQ (price, platforms, refund) |

A Page with three posts from last year looks dead. Post once a week (the best answer of the week, rewritten), or
hide the post count behind a strong pinned post.

## 3. Meta Business Suite (optional, free)
If you also use Instagram, connect both in Meta Business Suite so one inbox and one scheduler cover Facebook and
Instagram. Schedule with Meta's own tools or a scheduler that uses Meta's official API; never a browser bot
([08-rules-and-risks.md](08-rules-and-risks.md)).

## 4. Links: in the post or in the first comment?
Meta tested limiting Pages and professional-mode profiles to two links in organic posts a month unless they pay
for Meta Verified; links in comments were not limited, and publishers were excluded
([TechCrunch, Dec 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/17/facebook-is-testing-a-link-posting-limit-for-professional-accounts-and-pages)).
Check whether your account is in the test: if it is, put the link in the first comment. Either way, the link goes to
a page that delivers what the post promised.

## Checklist

```text
[ ] profile: real photo, one-line intro naming the product, Work → Page, featured link with UTM
[ ] Page: same name as store and landing, real screenshot cover, website with UTM, messaging + FAQ
[ ] pinned post: demo or before → after
[ ] (optional) Business Suite connected to Instagram
[ ] answer intro written: "I make <product>, so take this with that in mind"
```

# 06 · Your own group

Start one when about 20 real people use the product, or when you keep answering the same question in other
groups. Before that, an empty group looks worse than none.

## Name it after the job, not the product

| Option | Example (illustrative) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Job + who | `Mac Productivity for Beginners` | People search for the job; the group stays useful if the product changes |
| Product + users | `Clipnest Users` | Fine for support, but strangers won't find or join it |

> **Illustrative example.** Fictional product.

Many makers run both: a job-named group for reach, product support on the Page inbox or the help page.

## Set it up (30 minutes)
- **Description:** who it's for, what they'll get, that you made <product> and it's mentioned here sometimes.
- **Rules:** 5–7 short ones (be kind, stay on topic, no spam, promo only in the weekly thread, say if you made
  something). Third-party guides say Facebook allows up to 10 group rules
  ([Groupboss guide](https://groupboss.io/blog/facebook-group-rules)); check in your group settings.
- **Entry questions:** 2–3 ("What do you use your Mac for?", "What's the hardest part?"), plus an optional email
  question **only** with clear consent wording and a link to your privacy page
  ([email-list-and-deliverability](../../../skills/email/email-list-and-deliverability/SKILL.md)).
- **Post approval** for new members' first posts, if spam shows up. Admin tools can decline posts with links or
  keywords automatically (Admin Assist, per [Social Media Examiner](https://www.socialmediaexaminer.com/facebook-admin-assist-streamline-your-facebook-group-content-management/); check the current options in your group).

## The first 30 days

| Week | Do |
|---|---|
| 1 | Invite your current users and email list (no strangers, no "add all friends"). Pin a welcome post that asks people to introduce themselves |
| 2 | One how-to (F02) and one question (F08) a week; answer every post the same day |
| 3 | Start one weekly thread (e.g. "Show your setup Friday" or "Ask anything Monday") |
| 4 | Ask 2–3 active members to help moderate; read what they ask for: that's your roadmap |

## Keep it a group, not an ad feed
- At most 1 in 5 of your posts mention the product. The rest help.
- Announce updates once, with what changed for them.
- Never add people without asking; never buy members. Meta lists buying or exchanging engagement and trading
  groups or admin roles as spam ([Community Standards: Spam](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/spam/)).

# 04 · Groups: find, join, answer, post

Template: [templates/group-tracker.md](../templates/group-tracker.md). Skill:
[social-facebook-groups](../skills/social-facebook-groups/SKILL.md).

## 1. Find 10–20 groups

| Search | Example (illustrative) | Finds |
|---|---|---|
| The job your product does | `budget planner`, `wedding planning`, `invoice for freelancers` | People with the problem |
| The platform | `Mac users`, `iPhone tips`, `Notion templates` | People on your platform |
| The profession or hobby | `teachers <country>`, `photographers <city>`, `<game> players` | Your buyers, as a community |
| The local language | `phần mềm`, `ứng dụng`, `<job>` in Vietnamese / Thai / Spanish | Local buyers |
| Competitor names | `<competitor> users`, `<competitor> alternative` | People already paying for the job |

> **Illustrative example.** Search terms to show the method; run your own search.

## 2. Score them before you join

| Check | Good sign | Bad sign |
|---|---|---|
| Activity | Several posts a day, comments under them | Last post weeks ago, or only spam |
| Questions | "Which app…", "how do I…" threads in the last month | No questions, only selling or memes |
| Rules | Clear rules: a promo day, a monthly thread, or "ask admin" | "No promotion" with no exception, or no rules and a feed full of ads |
| Size | Big enough to have weekly questions | Huge and unmoderated |
| Language and country | Yours, or one you can write well | One you can't write |

Join the best 5 first. Answer entry questions honestly ("I make a Mac app and use Macs daily").

## 3. Answer (15–20 minutes a day)
Look for questions your product answers, and questions near it that you can answer well without it.

```text
1. Answer the question itself first: the steps, the free way, the setting they missed.
2. Add what you know from building in this space (a limit, a trick, a mistake to avoid).
3. Mention other tools if they're a better fit for this person.
4. Only then, if your product truly solves the rest: "I made <product> for this; it <does the last step>.
   Free / $X. <link with UTM, only if the rules allow links>."
5. Come back later and answer follow-ups.
```

### The 5-check test before you mention your product
Mention it only if all five are yes:
1. The person asked for a tool, or for something your product does in one step.
2. Your answer is useful even if they never click.
3. The group's rules allow it (or an admin said yes).
4. You say you made it, in the same sentence.
5. You haven't mentioned it in this group in the last week.

## 4. Post (one per group per month, at most)
After 2–4 weeks of answers, a post is welcome when it gives the group something.

| Format | When it works |
|---|---|
| How-to with steps written out | The group's common question, answered once, properly |
| "I made this for this group" + feedback request | Rules allow it or an admin approved it; you've helped there already |
| Before → after with screenshots | Visual result (design, photos, budgets, templates) |
| Free template / checklist | You give the thing in the post; the app is an optional extra |
| Build story with a real number | Groups of makers and small business owners |

Full formats: [05-post-formats.md](05-post-formats.md). Ask the admin when the rules don't say:
*"Hi, I've been answering questions here for a few weeks. I made a free <thing> that solves <common question>.
Could I share it once, in a post or in the promo thread, whichever you prefer?"*

## 5. Track
Every post and every answer that mentions the product gets a row in the
[group tracker](../templates/group-tracker.md), with its UTM `utm_campaign=<group-slug>`. After 30 days you know which
groups bring clicks and which only bring likes.

## What admins remove (and how to avoid it)
- Links from new members → answer for 2 weeks first.
- The same post in many groups → rewrite for each, one group per day at most.
- "DM me for the link" → link in the post or comment if allowed, or no link.
- Posting outside the promo thread or day → find it, use it.
- Arguing after removal → read the rule, thank the admin, move on.

# 07 · Metrics and stop rules

Template: [templates/weekly-numbers.md](../templates/weekly-numbers.md). 10 minutes every Sunday, a real review monthly.

## What to track (and where)

| Metric | Where | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| Answers given / product mentioned | Your [group tracker](../templates/group-tracker.md) | Effort, and how often the 5-check test passed |
| Reactions and replies on your answers and posts | The group | Whether people find you useful |
| Posts declined or removed | The group, your notifications | Whether you read the rules |
| Profile and Page visits | Page insights (Meta Business Suite) | Whether people want to know who you are |
| Clicks `utm_source=facebook` | GA4 / Plausible, by `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` (= group) | Which group and format bring visits |
| Signups / trials / installs | Your analytics, checkout, store | Whether Facebook brings **buyers** |
| Own group: members, weekly active posters | Group insights | Whether the community lives on its own |

UTM pattern: `utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=group|page|profile|messenger&utm_campaign=<group-slug or post>&utm_content=<F-code>`.

## Stop and fix rules
- **Answers get no reactions after 2 weeks:** wrong groups or answers too short. Re-score the groups; answer the
  question fully before anything else.
- **Posts declined or removed:** read the rule you broke, ask the admin how to share next time, wait a month there.
- **Reactions but no profile visits:** your name isn't connected to anything. Fix the profile intro and Work link.
- **Profile visits but no clicks:** the profile or Page doesn't say what the product is. One line, one link.
- **Clicks but no signups:** the landing doesn't match what the group was promised. Same words at the top of the
  page, the free thing first.
- **30 days of daily answers, 3+ posts, still no tracked signups:** this group set isn't your market. Try 5 new
  groups once; if the next 30 days are the same, move the time to another channel (see the
  [fit test](01-strategy.md#fit-test-yes-test-or-no)).

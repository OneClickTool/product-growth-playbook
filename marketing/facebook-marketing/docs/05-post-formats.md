# 05 · Post formats: 8 kinds of post for software

One post, one format, one group (or your Page). Post file template: [templates/post-file.md](../templates/post-file.md).

| # | Format | What's in it | Best place | Link |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F01 | **Answer** | A full reply to someone's question | Any group, daily | Only if the 5-check test passes |
| F02 | **How-to** | 3–7 steps written out, 1–3 screenshots | Groups where the question repeats | End of post or first comment |
| F03 | **Before → after** | Two images, one sentence each | Visual groups (design, photo, budgets) | First comment |
| F04 | **Free thing** | The template / checklist / guide itself, attached or linked | Groups that allow free resources | The download page, with email optional |
| F05 | **Feedback request** | "I made this for people like you; what's missing?" + 1 screenshot | With admin OK, after weeks of answers | One link, disclosed |
| F06 | **Build story** | What you built, why, one real number, one lesson | Maker and small-business groups, your profile | First comment |
| F07 | **Short video / Reel** | 15–60 s of the result appearing, captions on | Your Page and profile; groups that allow video | In the post text |
| F08 | **Poll / question** | "How do you do <job> today?" with real options | Your own group, groups that allow polls | None: it's research |

## Writing it

```text
First line:   the problem or result in the group's words, no product name
Body:         the steps / the story / the two images; specific, local language, no hype
Disclosure:   "I made <product>" in the post, not hidden in a comment
Link:         https://your-site/page?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=group&utm_campaign=<group-slug>&utm_content=<F-code>
Ask:          a real question that invites comments ("What do you use for X?"), never "like if you agree"
```

Never: "comment YES and I'll send the link", "share to unlock", tagging strangers, or the same text in many groups.
Meta treats gating and engagement bait as spam or low quality ([03](03-how-feed-distribution-works.md)).

## Example: one fact, three posts

> **Illustrative example.** Fictional product and groups.

Fact: *Clipnest keeps every copy for 30 days, so a Mac beginner never loses a copied address again.*

| Where | Format | First line |
|---|---|---|
| "Mac for Beginners" group | F02 how-to | "Copied something, copied again, and lost the first one? Here's how to keep both on a Mac." |
| "Small Shop Owners VN" group (Vietnamese) | F06 build story | "Mình làm app này vì cứ copy địa chỉ khách là mất…" |
| Clipnest Page | F07 Reel | "Every copy, kept. 12 seconds." |

Three posts, three groups, three `utm_campaign` values, three different texts.

# 03 · How Facebook decides who sees a post, in plain words

Meta doesn't publish its full ranking. What follows is what Meta says on its own Transparency Center, then practice
from people who post for a living, labelled as such.

## What Meta says
- **Some content is shown less.** Meta lists what it reduces in Feed: "clickbait" and "engagement bait"; links to
  websites "covered with ads", slow to load or broken; comments "repeatedly copied and pasted"; limited-originality
  content mostly reposted from other sources; and content from creators who "repeatedly violate" its policies
  ([Reducing the distribution of problematic content](https://transparency.meta.com/enforcement/taking-action/lowering-distribution-of-problematic-content), updated April 2025).
- **People can turn some of this up or down.** Feed Preferences let users increase or switch off many of these
  demotions (same page). Your post doesn't reach everyone the same way.
- **Meta trimmed its detailed guidelines in 2025.** Several older Content Distribution Guidelines (for example on
  domains with limited original content and "click-gap") were removed in July 2025
  ([CDG changes log](https://transparency.meta.com/features/approach-to-ranking/cdgs-changes-corrections/)). Read
  old "Facebook algorithm" articles with that in mind.
- **Gating and fake engagement are spam, not just demoted.** "Like/share-gating", buying likes or follows, and
  links that promise one thing and deliver another break the spam policy
  ([Community Standards: Spam](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/spam/)).

## Practice to test (not Meta's words)
- **Groups beat Pages for a new maker.** A post in an active group is shown to members who engage with that group;
  a new Page has few followers to show it to. Your daily time goes to groups.
- **Comments in the first hour matter.** Reply to every comment on your post quickly and with substance; it keeps
  the conversation, and the post, alive.
- **Native beats a bare link.** Posts that show the thing (a screenshot, a short video, the steps written out) tend
  to travel further than a link with one line. Put the steps in the post, the link at the end or in a comment.
- **Same text in ten groups looks like spam to members, admins and Meta.** Rewrite for each group, and space them out.
- **Local language wins in local groups.** An English post in a Vietnamese group reads like an ad.

## So, in one line
Help in groups where your buyers already talk, show the thing in the post itself, never gate or bait, link to a
page that keeps the promise, and answer every comment.

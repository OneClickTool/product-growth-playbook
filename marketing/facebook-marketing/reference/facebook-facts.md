# Facebook facts, with sources

Checked 2026-10-13. Official = Meta's own policy, help, developer, newsroom or investor pages. Secondary = news,
third-party guides or tools; treat as a hint and verify before relying on it.

## Rules

| Fact | Source | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Spam includes: posting, engaging or creating accounts, Groups, Pages at "very high frequencies"; buying or exchanging likes, shares, views, follows, clicks (incl. engagement giveaways); like/share-gating; links that promise one thing and deliver another, cloaking, deceptive redirects; trading accounts, Groups, Pages or admin roles. Clearly identified branded content is an exception | [Meta Community Standards: Spam](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/spam/) | Official |
| Meta reduces Feed distribution of clickbait, engagement bait, links to sites covered with ads / slow / broken, copied-and-pasted comments, limited-originality content, misinformation, and content from repeat violators; users can adjust many demotions in Feed Preferences (updated April 25, 2025) | [Reducing the distribution of problematic content](https://transparency.meta.com/enforcement/taking-action/lowering-distribution-of-problematic-content) | Official |
| Several Content Distribution Guidelines were removed in July 2025 (e.g. domains with limited original content, click-gap, inauthentic sharing, low-quality comments as a separate guideline) | [CDG changes, corrections and adjustments](https://transparency.meta.com/features/approach-to-ranking/cdgs-changes-corrections/) | Official |
| Automated collection (scraping) without permission is against Meta's terms | [How Meta combats scraping](https://about.fb.com/news/2021/04/how-we-combat-scraping/) | Official |
| Messaging: the person starts the conversation; standard 24-hour reply window | [Messenger Platform policy](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/messenger-platform/policy) | Official |

## Not verified (don't rely on)
- Reels length and file-size limits: third-party guides disagree (3–90 s vs no limit since 2025). Use 9:16 and check
  in the upload screen.
- "Best time to post" and "post X times a day" numbers: Meta publishes none.
- Any claim that links in the post body are always demoted: Meta's pages describe link *quality*, not links as such.

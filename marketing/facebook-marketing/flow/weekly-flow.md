# Weekly Facebook flow for an indie dev

Total: 15–20 minutes a day + one 30-minute post slot a week + 10 minutes on Sunday. The why:
[docs/01-strategy.md](../docs/01-strategy.md).

Facebook groups reward being there often and helping, so this runs on a short daily habit, not a weekly batch.

## Every day (15–20 min)

| Minutes | Do | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Open your 5 groups (notifications on for "new posts" in the best 2); look for questions from today | 2–3 threads |
| 10–12 | Answer them fully (`/social-facebook reply <thread>`); product only if the 5-check test passes | 2–3 answers |
| 2 | Note any product mention in the [group tracker](../templates/group-tracker.md); reply to follow-ups from yesterday | Tracker updated |

## Once a week (≈30 min)
- **One post** in one group whose rules allow it and where you've answered for 2+ weeks
  (`/social-facebook post <group>`). Never more than one post per group per month.
- **One Page post**: the week's best answer, rewritten as a how-to (F02) or a short video (F07).
- **Own group** (if you have one): the weekly thread, plus answering every new post.

## Sunday (≈10 min)
Fill [weekly numbers](../templates/weekly-numbers.md) from your tracker, Page insights and site analytics; apply the
[stop rules](../docs/07-metrics-and-stop-rules.md) at the monthly review.

## Once a month (≈30 min)
- Sort groups by tracked clicks and signups. Keep the top 3, drop the bottom 2, find 2 new ones.
- Re-read the rules of every group you post in; rules change.
- Check every link loads without redirects and still has the UTM.

## Daily checklist (copy into your notes)

```text
[ ] 2–3 answers, each useful without a click
[ ] product mentioned only after the 5-check test, with "I made it"
[ ] no link where the rules forbid it · UTM on every link
[ ] tracker updated · yesterday's follow-ups answered
```

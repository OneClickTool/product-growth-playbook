# 08 · Rules and risks

Quotes from [Pinterest Community Guidelines](https://policy.pinterest.com/en/community-guidelines) unless noted.
Checked 2026-10-13; read the page again before relying on it.

## What counts as spam

| Rule (Pinterest's words) | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| "Don't create or save content that is repetitive, deceptive, or irrelevant in an attempt to make money." | New image per pin; don't save the same pin again and again |
| "Don't link to websites that are unsafe, deceptive, untrustworthy, unoriginal…" | Link to your own useful page, not a thin page made only to redirect |
| Links with "excessive or deceptive redirection" may be blocked | No link shorteners chained to other shorteners; link straight to the page |
| "Don't use automation that hasn't been explicitly approved by Pinterest." | Schedule natively or with a scheduler that uses Pinterest's official API; no bots, no browser scripts |
| Creating accounts "en masse" is prohibited | One brand account. A personal account is fine; a farm is not |
| "Don't send repeated, unsolicited messages…" | No mass DMs or comment spam |
| Generating "inauthentic traffic" is prohibited | No buying saves, followers or clicks |

## AI images
Pinterest's policies cover "synthetically generated content" (same page). Since 2025 Pinterest adds an "AI modified"
label to image pins it detects as AI-generated or edited, shown in close-up, with an appeal process
([Medianama, May 2025](https://www.medianama.com/2025/05/223-pinterest-adds-ai-modified-labels-to-images-globally/), secondary).
Use AI for backgrounds if you like; never present an AI image as a real customer, a real result or a real photo.

## Affiliate and own-product links
The guidelines don't single out affiliate links; the rule is that money-making must not be misleading. Say plainly
when a page sells your own product (or earns a commission), and follow the FTC in the US.

## What to do when…

| Symptom | Likely cause | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| "Link blocked" on your domain | A redirect, a shortener, or a page Pinterest flags | Link the final URL directly; check the page loads without redirects; appeal through Pinterest Help |
| Pins don't appear in search | New account, or broad words | Keep pinning fresh images weekly; tighten keywords; give it weeks |
| Account suspended | Spam signals: repeats, automation, many accounts | Stop every automated tool; appeal via Pinterest Help; describe what you changed |
| Pin labelled "AI modified" wrongly | Detection error | Appeal through the label |

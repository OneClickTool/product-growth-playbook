# 06 · Pin variants: 8 kinds of pin for software

Make 3–5 pins per page, each a different variant. Pin file template: [templates/pin-file.md](../templates/pin-file.md).

| # | Variant | Image | Headline formula | Best destination |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P01 | **Result** | The finished thing: planner page, clean desktop, edited photo | `<Result> in <time / steps>` | Landing or template page |
| P02 | **Before → after** | Split image, same frame | `From <mess> to <result>` | Landing |
| P03 | **How-to steps** | 3–5 numbered steps with small screenshots | `How to <task> (<n> steps)` | How-to page on your site |
| P04 | **Checklist / printable** | A checklist or printable preview | `<Topic> checklist (free)` | Free download page with email box |
| P05 | **List of ideas** | Grid of 4–6 options | `<n> <topic> ideas for <who>` | Blog list post |
| P06 | **Comparison** | Two columns, plain facts | `<Tool A> vs <Tool B> for <job>` | Honest comparison page |
| P07 | **Template preview** | The template filled with realistic sample data | `Free <tool> template for <who>` | Template page |
| P08 | **Short video** | 6–15 s screen capture of the result appearing | Same as P01 or P03 | Same as P01 or P03 |

## Writing the title and description

```text
Title:        <search term first>: <promise>                 (≤ 100 chars, the first 40 carry it)
Description:  1–2 sentences on who it's for and what they get, the main term + 1–2 related terms said naturally,
              a plain call to action ("Get the free template", "See how it works"). No hashtag walls.
Link:         https://your-site/page?utm_source=pinterest&utm_medium=pin&utm_campaign=<page>&utm_content=<variant>
```

## Example set for one page

> **Illustrative example.** Fictional product and page.

Page: `example.com/notion-study-planner` (free Notion template; the app syncs it to the phone).

| Pin | Variant | Title | Board |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | P07 | Notion study planner template: free, weekly + exam view | Notion Study Planner Templates |
| 2 | P03 | How to plan a study week in Notion (4 steps) | Study Tips for University |
| 3 | P02 | From messy to-do list to a calm study week | Study Planner Ideas |
| 4 | P04 | Exam week checklist (free printable) | Study Tips for University |

Four pins, four images, one page, four `utm_content` values. A month later, the one with the most outbound clicks
gets two new siblings.

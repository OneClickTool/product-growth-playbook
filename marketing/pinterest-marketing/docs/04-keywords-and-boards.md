# 04 · Keywords and boards

Template: [templates/keyword-sheet.md](../templates/keyword-sheet.md). About 30 minutes per topic.

## Where the words come from

| Source | How | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Pinterest search autocomplete | Type your topic in the Pinterest search bar, note every suggestion; then add a letter (a, b, c…) | The phrases people actually type |
| Guided search chips | Search a term, note the word chips under the bar | Narrower versions of the term |
| [Pinterest Trends](https://trends.pinterest.com/) (business account, browser only) | Search your term, pick your region, look at the 12-month curve and related terms | Is it rising, seasonal or flat; when to pin |
| Your own analytics | Search Console queries and top pages for your site | Topics you already rank for |
| Reviews and comments | Words buyers use in your app's reviews and in Reddit threads | Problem words, not feature words |

Trends facts (business account, up to two years of history, seasonal flags, not in the mobile app):
[Pinterest Help: Trends](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/pinterest-trends).

## Pick 3 terms per page

| Kind | Example (illustrative) | Use it in |
|---|---|---|
| Main term | `study planner` | Board name, title start |
| Narrower term | `study planner printable`, `notion study planner` | Title, description |
| Problem term | `how to stop procrastinating studying` | Description, a how-to pin |

> **Illustrative example.** Terms shown to explain the format; run your own search.

Skip terms that only bring inspiration you can't serve (e.g. `aesthetic desk` for a CLI tool).

## Boards
- **Name = search term.** `Notion Study Planner Templates`, not `Our Templates`.
- **5–10 boards** to start; each with 1–2 sentences of description using the terms.
- **One pin, one best board.** Save it first to the most specific board.
- **Sections** inside a board for sub-topics once a board has 30+ pins.
- **Keep it relevant.** A few boards of other people's good pins in your niche make the profile useful; never filler.

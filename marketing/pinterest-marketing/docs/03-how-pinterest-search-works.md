# 03 · How Pinterest search works, in plain words

Pinterest doesn't publish its ranking. What follows is what Pinterest says in its own help pages, then practice
from people who pin for a living, labelled as such.

## What Pinterest says
- **Titles and descriptions help delivery.** Pinterest's specs page says descriptions don't show in home or search
  feeds but "help the algorithm with delivery", and only the first ~40 characters of a title show in feeds
  ([Pinterest Help: product specs](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/pinterest-product-specs)).
- **Shape matters.** 2:3 is the recommended ratio; taller pins may be cut off in feeds (same source).
- **It counts what people do.** Impressions, saves, pin clicks and outbound clicks are the metrics Pinterest reports
  ([Pinterest Help: Analytics](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/pinterest-analytics)). Treat them as the
  signals the platform watches too.
- **Search and saves drive Trends.** The Trends tool is built from searches and saves
  ([Pinterest Help: Trends](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/pinterest-trends)).
- **Repetitive saving is spam.** "Don't create or save content that is repetitive"
  ([Community Guidelines](https://policy.pinterest.com/en/community-guidelines)).

## Practice to test (not Pinterest's words)
- **Fresh images beat re-saves.** Scheduling tools define "the same pin" as the same image with the same link and
  advise new images for the same page ([Tailwind help](https://support.tailwindapp.com/en/articles/13853289-what-does-it-mean-to-post-duplicate-pins-on-pinterest-what-does-pinterest-consider-fresh-content)).
- **Consistency over bursts.** A few pins spread across the week, every week, is the common advice; there is no
  published number.
- **Slow start.** New pins and new accounts often take weeks to collect search impressions. Judge at 90 days.
- **Seasonal terms rise early.** Pin seasonal topics before the peak Trends shows, not on the day.

## So, in one line
Pick a search term people really type, put it in the board name, the first 40 characters of the title and the
description, show the result in a clear 2:3 image, link to a page that delivers it, and keep making new pins.

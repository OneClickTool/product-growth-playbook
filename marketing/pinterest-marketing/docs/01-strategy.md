# 01 · Strategy: is Pinterest for your product?

## Why Pinterest can work for small software
- **People come to plan, and they search.** Pinterest's Trends tool is built on searches and saves: it shows which
  search terms rise each week, month and year, and which are seasonal
  ([Pinterest Help: Trends](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/pinterest-trends)). That's demand you can
  read before you make anything.
- **A pin links out.** Every pin can carry a destination link, and Pinterest counts "outbound clicks" as its own
  metric ([Pinterest Help: Analytics](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/pinterest-analytics)). On most
  feeds a link is a fight; on Pinterest it's the normal thing to click.
- **It's big.** Pinterest reported 640 million monthly active users for Q2 2026
  ([Pinterest Q2 2026 results, SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001506293/000150629326000102/q2-26xpressrelease.htm)).
- **A pin isn't tied to today.** Search, not a feed of the last hour, decides most of who sees it. Plan for months.

## Fit test: yes, test, or no

| Signal | Points to **yes** | Points to **no** |
|---|---|---|
| Can one tall image show the result? | A planner page, a desk setup, a wallpaper, a template, a before/after | A CLI flag, an API, a background daemon |
| Do your buyers plan things? | Students, creators, home, wellness, small shops, teachers, families | Developers fixing a bug right now |
| Do you have pages worth landing on? | How-to guides, free templates, a gallery, a landing with a clear demo | Only an App Store link and a changelog |
| Search check | Your topic shows autocomplete suggestions on Pinterest and appears in Trends | Search shows nothing related |

- 3–4 yes → Pinterest can be a main channel. 2 → **test** for 90 days as a support channel. 0–1 → skip; see the
  [marketing hub](../../README-marketing-social.md#3--which-channel-first).
- Good fits for software: Notion / spreadsheet / Canva templates, planners and habit apps, study apps, photo and
  design tools, wallpaper and theme apps, recipe and meal-plan apps, budgeting apps, Mac desk-setup and productivity
  tools with a visual result. Weak fits: dev tools, B2B infrastructure.

## The plan: three phases

| Phase | Weeks | Do | Don't | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 · Set up** | 1 | Business account, claimed website, 5–10 boards named for searches, 3 landing pages picked | Pin before the pages exist | Every board has a search-word name and a description |
| **2 · Seed** | 2–8 | 5–10 new pins a week, 3–5 different pins per page, spread over the week | Save the same image again and again, or post 50 in a day | Pins get impressions from search, saves start |
| **3 · Double down** | 9+ | Each month, make new pins for the pages and topics that got outbound clicks; add pages for rising Trends terms | Judge a pin on its first week | You know your 3 best pages and 3 best pin variants |

## Pin to page, not pin to store
App stores strip most tracking and give a stranger one shot. A how-to page or free template on **your** site gives
the reader what the pin promised, and offers the app as the faster way, plus an email box. That's also where
[claiming your website](02-profile-setup.md) pays off: your name shows on pins saved from your pages.

## How Pinterest fits the other channels
Pinterest is reach for planners and makers of things; it sends people to your pages, not to a profile. Reuse the
same visual facts as your Instagram carousels and TikTok slideshows, re-cut to 2:3 with a search-word title. The
full map: [Marketing & Social](../../README-marketing-social.md).

# Weekly Pinterest flow for an indie dev

Total: ~1 hour a week in one sitting + 10 minutes on Sunday + 45 minutes once a month. The why:
[docs/01-strategy.md](../docs/01-strategy.md).

Pinterest rewards steady, fresh pins more than daily attention, so it runs on a weekly batch, not a daily routine.

## Once a week (≈60 min, one sitting)

| Minutes | Do | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | Pick 2–3 pages to pin this week (new pages first, then last month's winners) and their terms from the [keyword sheet](../templates/keyword-sheet.md) | Pages + terms |
| 35 | Make 5–10 pins: different [variants](../docs/06-pin-variants.md) per page, 2:3, from your master files (`/social-pinterest pin <URL>`) | Images + [pin files](../templates/pin-file.md) |
| 15 | Schedule them across the week, each to its best board, each link with UTM | 5–10 pins queued |

## Sunday (≈10 min)
Fill [weekly numbers](../templates/weekly-numbers.md) from Pinterest Analytics and your site analytics; apply the
[stop rules](../docs/07-metrics-and-stop-rules.md) only at the monthly review.

## Once a month (≈45 min)
- Sort last month's pins by outbound clicks. Top 3 pages → make 2 new pins each next month.
- Check Pinterest Trends for your terms: anything rising or seasonal in the next 1–3 months gets a page and pins now.
- Rename or merge boards that get no impressions; add one board for a new term.
- Check every destination link loads without redirects and still has the UTM.

## Weekly checklist (copy into your notes)

```text
[ ] 2–3 pages picked, terms from the keyword sheet
[ ] 5–10 new pin images (no re-saves), each a different variant
[ ] title starts with the term · description with 1–2 related terms · link with UTM
[ ] scheduled across the week, each to its best board
[ ] Sunday numbers filled
```

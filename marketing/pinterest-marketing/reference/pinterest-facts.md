# Pinterest facts, with sources

Checked 2026-10-13. Official = Pinterest's own help, policy, business or investor pages. Secondary = news,
third-party guides or tools; treat as a hint and verify before relying on it.

## Pins

| Fact | Source | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Image pins: PNG or JPEG, recommended 1000 × 1500 px (2:3); taller than 2:3 may be cut off in feeds; up to 20 MB desktop / 32 MB app | [Pinterest Help: product specs](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/pinterest-product-specs) | Official (ads specs) |
| Title up to 100 characters, about the first 40 show in feeds; description up to 800 characters, not shown in feeds, "help the algorithm with delivery" | same | Official (ads specs) |
| Video pins: MP4 / MOV / M4V, 4 s to 15 min; carousels 2–5 images at 1:1 or 2:3 | same | Official (ads specs) |
| Creative: vertical 2:3, logo visible but not dominant, key message top / details bottom, a clear CTA; description up to 500 characters | [Pinterest Business: creative best practices](https://business.pinterest.com/creative-best-practices/) | Official |

## Analytics

| Fact | Source | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Impressions = times on screen; saves = saves to a board; pin clicks = opens in close-up; outbound clicks = actions leading off Pinterest, counted from the date the link was added | [Pinterest Help: Analytics](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/pinterest-analytics) | Official |

## Rules

| Fact | Source | Type |
|---|---|---|
| No repetitive, deceptive or irrelevant content to make money; no unsafe or deceptive links; no deceptive redirection; no unapproved automation; no mass account creation; no inauthentic traffic | [Pinterest Community Guidelines](https://policy.pinterest.com/en/community-guidelines) | Official |
| "AI modified" labels on image pins detected as AI-generated or edited, shown in close-up, with appeals (rolled out 2025) | [Medianama, May 2025](https://www.medianama.com/2025/05/223-pinterest-adds-ai-modified-labels-to-images-globally/) | Secondary |

## Not verified (don't rely on)
- How far ahead the native scheduler lets you schedule, and how many pins it holds: third-party guides disagree
  (two weeks vs 30 days, 10 vs 100 pins). Check in your account.
- "Pin X times a day" numbers: Pinterest publishes none.
- Any claim that a hashtag count, a posting hour or a board count changes reach.

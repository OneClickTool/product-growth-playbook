# 💰 Monetization

Decide what to charge, what's free, how to ask for the money, and how to actually get paid.

| Skill | What you get | Level | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| [monetization-free-trial-vs-freemium](monetization-free-trial-vs-freemium/SKILL.md) | The right model, the free/paid line and upgrade triggers | Intermediate | 30–45 min |
| [monetization-pricing-strategy](monetization-pricing-strategy/SKILL.md) | Monthly and yearly price, what you keep after fees, a test plan | Intermediate | 60 min |
| [monetization-payment-setup](monetization-payment-setup/SKILL.md) | A provider that works from your country, a verification pack, your first payout | Beginner → Intermediate | 1–3 h + review |
| [monetization-in-app-purchase-setup](monetization-in-app-purchase-setup/SKILL.md) | Your IAP route (StoreKit 2 + Play Billing, a free wrapper, or RevenueCat & co.), a working sandbox purchase, an App Review checklist | Intermediate → Advanced | 2 h – 3 days |
| [monetization-paywall-design](monetization-paywall-design/SKILL.md) | Paywall copy and layout, placements, compliance checklist | Intermediate | 45–60 min |
| [monetization-subscription-tiers](monetization-subscription-tiers/SKILL.md) | 2–3 tiers with personas, fences and prices | Advanced | 45–60 min |

**Suggested order:** payment setup (start early, reviews take days) → in-app purchase setup (mobile / Mac App Store) · trial vs freemium → pricing → paywall → tiers (once you have paying users).

**Open for contributors:** pricing experiments. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md).

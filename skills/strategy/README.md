# 🧭 Strategy

The thinking that decides what all the other skills are for: a money target with a deadline, where to spend and when
to push, moves your competitors aren't making, and how each product builds a long-term audience.

| Skill | What you get | Level | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| [strategy-revenue-target](strategy-revenue-target/SKILL.md) | "$X in N months" worked backward into a weekly funnel, a reality check and go / fix / kill rules (with a script) | Beginner | 45–60 min |
| [strategy-spend-and-timing](strategy-spend-and-timing/SKILL.md) | Costs sorted into must-pay / pays-back / skip, the one bottleneck to fund, momentum and slump signals, a 2-week finishing sprint | Intermediate | 45 min + 15 min/week |
| [strategy-contrarian-marketing](strategy-contrarian-marketing/SKILL.md) | A map of what the herd does, inverted moves through 6 lenses, and 3 honest experiments with pass numbers | Intermediate | 60 min |
| [strategy-product-as-funnel](strategy-product-as-funnel/SKILL.md) | Each product's give → get, one capture point at the moment of value, honest cross-promotion, owned-audience tracking | Intermediate | 60–90 min |

**Suggested order:** revenue target (once) → product as funnel (once per product set) → contrarian marketing (when the
usual channels stall) → spend and timing (every Monday).

**Not covered yet:** fundraising, hiring, market sizing. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md).

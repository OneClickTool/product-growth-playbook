# 🧪 Case Study

Learn from what already happened: your competitors' choices, other founders' public stories, and your own experiments.

| Skill | What you get | Level | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| [case-study-competitor-teardown](case-study-competitor-teardown/SKILL.md) | **Learn from the enemy:** 3 competitors torn down with free tools, then a Copy / Beat / Avoid list | Intermediate | 2–3 h |
| [case-study-success-story-analysis](case-study-success-story-analysis/SKILL.md) | 5 comparable successes and 2 failures, bias-checked, then 3 tactics to try this month | Beginner | 60–90 min |
| [case-study-write-your-own](case-study-write-your-own/SKILL.md) | Your results as an honest one-page case study, ready to share or contribute | Beginner | 45–60 min |

**Suggested order:** competitor teardown → success stories → (run your own experiment) → write your own case study.

**Share your case study.** Real numbers from real products are the most valuable thing in this repo.
Follow [case-study-write-your-own](case-study-write-your-own/SKILL.md) and open a pull request or an issue.

> 📖 Tip: keep teardowns and case studies as Markdown in your research folder, and open them in [Markdown Viewer](https://oneclicktool.app/macos/markdown-viewer?utm_source=github&utm_medium=area-readme&utm_campaign=growth-playbook&utm_content=case-study)
> (free, Mac, made by the maintainer) to see how they link to your notes and decisions.

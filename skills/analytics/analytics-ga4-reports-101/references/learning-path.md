# Learning path: 7 short lessons

Each lesson takes 10–20 minutes, uses **your own** property, and ends with one line you write in
`growth/analytics-notes.md`. Do one a day, or all in one sitting. Meanings are in
[metrics-glossary.md](metrics-glossary.md).

| # | Lesson | Open | Do this in your property | Write down |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Is data arriving? | Reports → Realtime | Open your site on your phone (mobile data, not office Wi-Fi). Find yourself by city and by `page_view` | "Realtime works: yes/no" and whether the internal filter hides office visits |
| 2 | Where visits come from | Acquisition → Traffic acquisition | Last 28 days, compare to preceding period. Sort by sessions | Top 3 channels with sessions and engagement rate |
| 3 | Who is new | Acquisition → User acquisition | Same range. Compare its top channel with lesson 2 | One sentence: "New people mostly find me via X; visits mostly come via Y" |
| 4 | Doors into the site | Engagement → Landing page | Sort by sessions, then by key events | The top door, and the page with the best session key event rate |
| 5 | Did they do the thing? | Engagement → Events, then the *Key events* column in lesson 2 and 4 | Check your key event fires; see which channel has the most key events | Key events by top 3 channels |
| 6 | What people search | Search Console → Performance → Queries, then Pages | Last 28 days. Sort by impressions. Find queries with high impressions, CTR under 2%, position 5–20 | Three queries worth a better title or a new section |
| 7 | Cross two things | Explore → Free form | Rows: Landing page. Columns: Session default channel group. Values: Sessions, Key events | Which channel × page pair has the most key events |

## After lesson 7

You have all inputs for the baseline note in the [reports-101 prompt](../SKILL.md#prompt-copy-paste). Write it,
date it, and repeat lessons 2, 4, 5 and 6 every week (15 minutes).

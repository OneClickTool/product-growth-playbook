# Step 7: Weekly rhythm and monthly review

**Week 6 onwards** · [← Step 6](06-community-posting.md) · Next: [Step 8](08-paid-ads-gate.md)

## Goal
A schedule small enough to keep for months. Long-term users come from consistency, not from one big launch.

## Weekly (about 5–6 hours)

> **Illustrative example.** Adjust the hours to your own week.

| When | What | Step |
|---|---|---|
| Monday, 30 min | Read last week's numbers; move time to the channel that brought installs | 1 |
| Twice a week, 1 h | Produce 2–3 short videos from the winning template | 5 |
| Once a week, 1 h | One community post (Reddit **or** an on-target group) | 6 |
| Daily, 15 min | Answer existing questions; substantive replies on X | 6 |
| Monthly, 1 h | One email to your list; one ASO change or review | 2, 3 |

## Monthly review
Write one row in `growth/monthly.md`:

| Month | Installs by channel | Store conversion | New email contacts | Paying users | Kept / stopped |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Then ask:
- Which channel brought **activated** users (not just clicks)? Give it more time next month.
- Which channel brought nothing for 4 weeks? Stop it or change one thing.
- Did anything suddenly work? Double down within days:
  [strategy-spend-and-timing](../../strategy/strategy-spend-and-timing/SKILL.md).
- Got a real result? Write it up: [case-study-write-your-own](../../case-study/case-study-write-your-own/SKILL.md).

## Done when
The weekly schedule is in your calendar and the first monthly row is written.

# Step 2: Turn your other products into a funnel

**Weeks 1–2 · the highest-leverage step if you already have live products** · [← Step 1](01-measure-first.md) · Next: [Step 3](03-aso-one-change-at-a-time.md)

## Goal
Users who already installed something from you (browser extensions, other apps, a free tool) hear about the hero
product at the right moment, and leave an email so you can reach them for every future launch.

Full method: [strategy-product-as-funnel](../../strategy/strategy-product-as-funnel/SKILL.md).

## Steps
- [ ] **List your live products** with weekly active users and who uses them, in `growth/audience.md`.
- [ ] **Match audiences.** Only products whose users overlap with the hero product's users get cross-promo.
      An unrelated audience gives clicks, not users.
- [ ] **Write "give → get" for each fitting product:** what value the user gets first, the moment they get it, and
      only then the ask (try the hero product, or leave an email).
- [ ] **Add the touchpoints**, one or two per product:
  - a small "More from the developer" link or section (popup, settings or about screen),
  - the welcome page after install and the "what's new" page after an update,
  - an email capture with a specific promise ("one email when a new tool ships").
- [ ] **Tag the source** on every link and every email sign-up, so you can see which product feeds which.
- [ ] **Check store rules** before shipping: no deceptive ads, no forced interstitials, no pre-ticked consent.
      See the [Chrome Web Store program policies](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/listing-requirements/)
      and the [App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/).
- [ ] **Email rules** (consent, double opt-in, deliverability):
      [email-list-and-deliverability](../../email/email-list-and-deliverability/SKILL.md).

## Done when
Every fitting product has a tagged cross-promo link and one capture point, and you can see clicks per product.

## Track monthly
New email contacts per product · capture rate (new contacts ÷ active users) · installs of the hero product from
cross-promo · unsubscribes.

## Common mistakes
- Asking for an email before the user got any value.
- Promoting the hero product inside every product regardless of audience.
- Collecting emails and never writing. One useful email a month keeps the list warm.

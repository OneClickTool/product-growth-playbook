# Step 4: Listings and a few high-intent pages

**Weeks 2–3 · do once, then maintain** · [← Step 3](03-aso-one-change-at-a-time.md) · Next: [Step 5](05-short-video.md)

## Goal
Be findable where people already look for tools like yours, without a blog-post treadmill.

## Steps
- [ ] **Platform listings.** Run Level 1 of the skill for your platform:
      [Mac app](../../listing/listing-mac-app/SKILL.md) ·
      [App Store](../../listing/listing-app-store/SKILL.md) ·
      [browser extension](../../listing/listing-browser-extension/SKILL.md) ·
      [AI product](../../listing/listing-ai-product/SKILL.md) ·
      [dev tool](../../listing/listing-dev-plugin/SKILL.md).
- [ ] **Free directories**, Level 1 only for now: [listing-free-directories](../../listing/listing-free-directories/SKILL.md).
- [ ] **Log every listing** in `growth/listings.md`: site, URL, status, date, tracked link used.
- [ ] **3–5 high-intent pages** on your own site instead of many blog posts. Each answers one search from someone
      ready to install:
  - "<competitor> alternative for <platform>"
  - "how to <job your product does> on <platform>"
  - "<job> without <annoying thing>"

  Find the real phrasing first: [research-where-users-ask](../../research/research-where-users-ask/SKILL.md).
  Each page: the problem, the steps (with and without your product), a demo, the install button.

## Done when
Listed on the main sites for your platform, and 3–5 intent pages are live and linked from the landing page.

## Common mistakes
- Submitting to 100 low-quality directories in one day. Pick the ones your users actually browse.
- Writing generic "top 10 tips" posts that compete with big sites. Intent pages are narrower and convert better.

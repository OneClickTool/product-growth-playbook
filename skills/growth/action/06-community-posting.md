# Step 6: Community posting without getting removed

**Week 4 onwards · 1 post a week + 15 minutes a day** · [← Step 5](05-short-video.md) · Next: [Step 7](07-weekly-rhythm.md)

## Goal
Get users from Reddit, Facebook groups and X without spam, bans or burning your name.

## Reddit
Rules look scary because each subreddit has its own. Treat them as a checklist, not a wall.
- [ ] **Pick subreddits by product type** (showcase first, then audience, then developer subs for the build
      story): [launch-reddit-posting](../../launch/launch-reddit-posting/SKILL.md).
- [ ] **Fetch the current rules** before every post and save them in `growth/community.md`:
      ```bash
      python3 skills/launch/launch-reddit-posting/scripts/fetch_subreddit_rules.py <sub1> <sub2> -o growth/subreddit-rules.md
      ```
- [ ] **Two weeks of useful comments** in those subs before your first post, if your account is new there.
- [ ] **Answer the usual questions in the post itself**: price model, privacy, platform version, native or not,
      where to download.
- [ ] **One post a week at most**, each in a different sub or format. Reply to every comment within the first hours.
- [ ] Writing help: [launch-post-writing](../../launch/launch-post-writing/SKILL.md).

## Facebook groups
- [ ] **Groups whose members aren't your users** (large but off-target): don't drop product links. Use them to
      test hooks before making videos, recruit beta testers, or tell the build story with the link in a comment and
      a tracked UTM. If tracked installs stay at zero after 3 posts, stop.
- [ ] **Find on-target groups**: users of your platform, the job your product does, your users' profession.
- [ ] Follow each group's rules; ask an admin before the first product post.
- [ ] Tone: [social-credible-posting](../../social/social-credible-posting/SKILL.md).

## X
- [ ] With few followers, own posts get little reach. Spend the time on **replies with substance** under larger
      accounts in your niche, and post your winning short videos.
- [ ] Optional, long-term: [social-build-in-public](../../social/social-build-in-public/SKILL.md).

## "Seeding", the honest way
Seeding is **answering existing questions** ("is there an app that does X?"), not fake accounts praising
your product.
- [ ] Find where those questions are: [research-where-users-ask](../../research/research-where-users-ask/SKILL.md).
- [ ] 15 minutes a day: answer fully first, mention your product only if it truly fits, and say you made it.

## Done when
A rules snapshot is saved, one compliant post is live, and the daily answer habit has started.

## Common mistakes
- Posting the same text in 10 places on the same day.
- Hiding that you're the maker.
- Arguing with moderators after a removal. Read the rule, fix, move on.

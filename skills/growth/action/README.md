# Action plan: steady, long-term users without ads

A step-by-step order for a solo maker or small team whose product is **live** (App Store, direct download, web or a
browser store) but who feels stuck: blog SEO is slow, X has no reach, Reddit rules feel scary, ASO changes feel like
guessing, and there's no budget or experience for ads.

It doesn't name a product. Run each step against **your** product, and save the output in a `growth/` folder next
to your code so your agent can pick it up next session.

## The one rule: channels in order
Most people get stuck because they run 6 channels at once and can't tell which one works. Go in this order:

| Order | Channel type | Why this order |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Measurement** | Without per-channel links you can't tell what worked, so every later step is guessing. |
| 2 | **Owned** (users of your other products, email list) | Free, no community rules, and the users stay yours. |
| 3 | **Search** (ASO, directories, a few high-intent pages) | Set up once, keeps working for months. |
| 4 | **Attention** (short video, Reddit, X, Facebook groups) | Fast, but only pays off when steps 2–3 are ready to catch the visitors. |
| 5 | **Paid ads** | Only after you know your store or landing page conversion. |

## Steps

| # | Step | Weeks | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Measure first](01-measure-first.md) | 1 | Every channel has its own tracked link; baseline numbers written down |
| 2 | [Turn your other products into a funnel](02-owned-audience.md) | 1–2 | Cross-promo and one email capture point live in each fitting product |
| 3 | [ASO, one change at a time](03-aso-one-change-at-a-time.md) | 2–3 (then monthly) | Keywords chosen, one listing change shipped, review date set |
| 4 | [Listings and a few high-intent pages](04-listings-and-intent-pages.md) | 2–3 | Listed on the main directories for your platform; 3–5 intent pages live |
| 5 | [Short video, batch-produced](05-short-video.md) | 3–6 | 9 videos posted, one winning format kept |
| 6 | [Community posting without getting removed](06-community-posting.md) | 4 onwards | 1 compliant post a week; daily answers to existing questions |
| 7 | [Weekly rhythm and monthly review](07-weekly-rhythm.md) | 6 onwards | A fixed weekly schedule and a monthly numbers log |
| 8 | [Paid ads gate](08-paid-ads-gate.md) | when the gate passes | A small, capped test with a stop rule |

Steps 3 and 4 can run in parallel with step 2. Don't start step 5 before step 1 is done.

## Stop doing now
- Writing many blog posts hoping some rank. A few intent pages beat volume (step 4).
- Changing title, subtitle, keywords and screenshots at the same time (step 3).
- Dropping a bare product link into groups whose members aren't your users (step 6).
- Fake accounts or self-praise "seeding". It gets accounts banned and kills trust (step 6).
- Paying for ads before you can measure conversion (step 8).


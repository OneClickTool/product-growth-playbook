# Step 8: Paid ads gate

**Only when every box below is ticked** · [← Step 7](07-weekly-rhythm.md) · [Back to the action plan](README.md)

## Goal
Not running ads yet is the right call when you can't measure. Ads multiply what already works; they don't fix a
store page that doesn't convert.

## The gate
- [ ] Tracking from [step 1](01-measure-first.md) works end to end (click → install → activation or purchase).
- [ ] You know your store or landing page conversion from at least 4 weeks of organic data.
- [ ] Screenshots and title have had at least one round from [step 3](03-aso-one-change-at-a-time.md).
- [ ] You know what one user is worth to you (price × share who pay, or ad revenue per user), even roughly.
- [ ] You have money you can afford to lose on a learning test.

## First test
- [ ] Decide when and how much to spend: [strategy-spend-and-timing](../../strategy/strategy-spend-and-timing/SKILL.md).
- [ ] One channel only. For App Store products, check which placements [Apple Ads](https://ads.apple.com/app-store)
      supports for your platform at the time you run it.
- [ ] A fixed cap and a fixed end date, set before you start.
- [ ] A stop rule written in advance, for example: stop if cost per paying user is above what a user is worth.
- [ ] Log the result in `growth/monthly.md`, whether it worked or not.

## Common mistakes
- Boosting a social post "to see what happens", with no tracked link.
- Spreading a small budget across several ad networks, so none gets enough data.
- Keeping a losing campaign running because "it might pick up".

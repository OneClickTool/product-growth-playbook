# Step 3: ASO, one change at a time

**Weeks 2–3, then one change a month** · [← Step 2](02-owned-audience.md) · Next: [Step 4](04-listings-and-intent-pages.md)

## Goal
Stop guessing. ASO feels unpredictable when you change everything at once, because you can't tell which change
moved the numbers. Change **one** element, wait, compare, keep or revert.

Full route: [Playbook: ASO Optimization](../../../playbooks/aso-optimization.md).

## Steps
- [ ] **Keywords** (once): [aso-keyword-research](../../aso/aso-keyword-research/SKILL.md). Save the chosen set in
      `growth/keywords.md`.
- [ ] **Pick the weakest element first**, judged by your step 1 baseline:

      | Symptom | Fix first | Skill |
      |---|---|---|
      | Few store page views | Title, subtitle, keywords | [aso-title-subtitle-optimization](../../aso/aso-title-subtitle-optimization/SKILL.md) |
      | Views but few installs | Screenshots, first frame | [aso-screenshot-strategy](../../aso/aso-screenshot-strategy/SKILL.md) |
      | Installs but low rating | Review prompt, replies | [aso-review-and-rating-strategy](../../aso/aso-review-and-rating-strategy/SKILL.md) |

- [ ] **Ship one change** and log it: date, what changed, the numbers on that date.
- [ ] **Wait at least 2–4 weeks** before judging (store indexing and weekly cycles need time), then compare with the
      same length period before the change.
- [ ] **Keep or revert**, then pick the next element. One change a month is enough.

## Done when
One logged change is live with a review date in your calendar.

## Common mistakes
- Updating keywords, title and screenshots in one release, then not knowing what worked.
- Judging after 3 days.
- Copying a competitor's keywords without checking whether you can rank for them.

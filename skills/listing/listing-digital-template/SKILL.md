# Digital Template Listing (Notion Marketplace · Gumroad)

> Publish a free version where Notion users browse, sell the full version where checkout is easy, and let the free
> one feed the paid one.

## Goal
Get a template in front of people who are already searching for templates, and sell it without tax headaches or
leaked copies.

## When to use
- A Notion template (or planner, dashboard, spreadsheet, kit) is ready to share or sell.
- You're choosing between Notion Marketplace and Gumroad, or want to use both.
- **Not for:** apps or plugins → [listing-dev-plugin](../listing-dev-plugin/SKILL.md), [listing-app-store](../listing-app-store/SKILL.md).

## Inputs
- A finished template in a clean workspace, with a **"Start here" page** (how to use it in 3–5 steps).
- A cover image, 3–6 screenshots of real views, and optionally a walkthrough video of 60 seconds or less.
- `assets/template-listing-sheet.md` from this skill.

## Where to list

| | Notion Marketplace | Gumroad |
|---|---|---|
| Free templates | ✅ Anyone can publish (after review) | ✅ `$0+` / pay-what-you-want |
| Paid templates | Join the **waitlist**, get approved (can take months), onboard with **Stripe** (only in Stripe-supported countries). Others can list paid templates that link to an external checkout | ✅ Immediately |
| Fee | **8% + $0.40** per sale, +1% FX fee outside the US | **10% + $0.50** per direct sale · **30%** on sales that come through Gumroad **Discover** |
| Tax | Handled via Notion/Stripe | **Merchant of record since 1 Jan 2025**: Gumroad collects and remits sales tax/VAT |
| Review | Yes, usually a few business days | No listing review (risk review before Discover) |
| Audience | People browsing notion.com/templates | Your own traffic, plus Discover once eligible |
| Notes | USD pricing only. 14-day refunds remove the buyer's copies | Delivery via a post-purchase page or file |

Fees change, so confirm them on [Notion's selling guide](https://www.notion.com/help/selling-on-marketplace) and [Gumroad pricing](https://gumroad.com/pricing) before you price.

## Steps

### Level 1: Publish a free template (fastest way to get seen)
1. **Make a free version (30 min)** that's genuinely useful on its own: the core system, not a crippled demo.
2. **Share it for duplication in Notion** (Share → Publish → allow duplicate as template) and test the duplicate link
   in a second account.
3. **Set up your creator profile** in Notion (photo, bio, links), then **submit to Notion Marketplace**: name, link,
   category, language, screenshots, optional video.
4. **Write the listing (20 min):** a name with the search phrase ("Freelance CRM – Notion Template"), a one-line
   outcome, who it's for, what's inside (views and databases), and how to start.
5. **Follow the guidelines** (below) so it isn't rejected.

### Level 2: Sell the paid version
1. **Gumroad first** (no waitlist): create a product, add the cover, thumbnail and description, and set the price
   (or pay-what-you-want with a suggested price).
2. **Deliver safely:** put the Notion duplicate link **only in the post-purchase content**, never on the public page.
   Add a "Start here" guide and your support email.
3. **Apply to sell on Notion Marketplace** (waitlist → approval → Stripe). While you wait, list the paid template on
   Notion with an external checkout link to Gumroad, if the guidelines allow it for your region.
4. **Connect free → paid:** the free template's "Start here" page links to the full version (one line, not an ad wall).
5. **Price it** → [monetization-pricing-strategy](../../monetization/monetization-pricing-strategy/SKILL.md).
   Templates often sell well as a one-time purchase with free updates.

### Level 3: Grow
- **Gumroad Discover:** becomes available once your account has payout details, has passed a threshold of genuine
  sales and the risk review, and the product has a category, tags and ratings enabled. Discover sales cost 30%, but the buyers are new.
- **Bundles:** 3 templates for the price of 2, via Gumroad bundles or versions.
- **Affiliates:** Gumroad's affiliate feature lets creators and newsletters earn a cut for promoting your template.
- **Updates as marketing:** email buyers when you add features, which brings reviews and word of mouth.
- **More channels:** r/Notion and template roundups (read the self-promotion rules), a 30-second template tour on
  TikTok/Shorts → [launch-quick-download-wins](../../launch/launch-quick-download-wins/SKILL.md).

## Guideline mistakes that get Notion templates rejected
- **Duplicates or near-copies** of your own or others' templates ("flooding" the marketplace is prohibited).
- **Low effort:** a messy layout, no instructions, or outdated Notion features.
- **Implying Notion endorsement,** or misusing Notion's name or logo (follow the brand and trademark guidelines).
- **Ads, spam links,** or screenshots and descriptions that don't match what's inside.
- **Paid templates priced in anything other than USD,** or listings used just to drive sales elsewhere.

## Prompt (copy-paste)
Replace everything in `{{ }}`.

````text
You are a digital-template launch expert for Notion Marketplace and Gumroad.

Template: {{what it does, who it's for, main databases/views}}
Free version idea: {{or "help me decide"}}   Price idea: {{...}}   My country: {{...}}

1. Split it into a FREE version (useful on its own) and a PAID version (clear reasons to upgrade).
2. Write both listings: name with the search phrase, one-line outcome, who it's for, "what's inside" bullets,
   "how to start" (3 steps), 5 tags/categories, and captions for 5 screenshots.
3. Write the "Start here" page for buyers, including where the duplicate link goes and a support line.
4. Recommend where to list each version (Notion Marketplace, Gumroad, both) given my country's Stripe eligibility
   for Notion paid sales, and show the take-home per sale at my price (Notion 8% + $0.40, +1% FX outside the US;
   Gumroad 10% + $0.50 direct, 30% via Discover). Tell me to confirm current fees.
5. Check the listing against Notion's guidelines: no duplicates, no implied endorsement, USD pricing, accurate screenshots.
````

*Freelance CRM*: **free** version on Notion Marketplace (clients + projects databases). **Paid "Pro"** at $19 on Gumroad

## Common mistakes
- **Putting the duplicate link on the public page.** Anyone can copy the paid template for free.
- **No "Start here" page.** Confused buyers ask for refunds.
- **Waiting for Notion's paid approval to start selling.** Sell on Gumroad meanwhile.
- **A free version that's useless** (everything locked). It gets no traction and no upgrades.
- **Five near-identical templates.** Notion treats it as flooding, and buyers see it as low quality.

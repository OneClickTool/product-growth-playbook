# Free Directories & Alternative Stores

> Prepare one launch kit, then submit it in three waves: the big free platforms first, launch directories second,
> alternative stores and niche sites third. Track every submission in one sheet.

## Goal
Get found in more places for free: people browsing for new tools, "alternative to X" searches, other app stores,
and backlinks for your website. Spend the time on sites that fit your product, not on 100 random directories.

## When to use
- Launch week, or right after launch → pairs with [launch-product-hunt-launch](../../launch/launch-product-hunt-launch/SKILL.md).
- A product that's live but only on one store.
- **Not for:** the main App Store or Google Play listing → [listing-app-store](../listing-app-store/SKILL.md),
  [listing-google-play](../listing-google-play/SKILL.md).

## Inputs
- A live product with a URL people can try.
- `assets/launch-kit.md` filled once, and `assets/directory-tracker.csv`.
- Or use the **[Listing Desk](https://htmlpreview.github.io/?https://github.com/OneClickTool/product-growth-playbook/blob/main/skills/listing/listing-free-directories/assets/listing-desk.html)** (opens in your browser, no install): 75+ sites grouped by area and
  tier S/A/B/C, with filters, sorting, a status per site for each product and CSV export. Progress stays in your
  browser. Source: [`assets/listing-desk.html`](assets/listing-desk.html).

## The list
Free options and rules change often. Each row was checked on **2026-09-30**. Confirm pricing on the site before you
submit. "Free (queue)" means free with a waiting line, and many sites sell a paid option to skip it.

### Level 1: do these first (biggest free reach)

| Where | For | Free? | Link |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product Hunt | Any tech product | Free | [producthunt.com](https://www.producthunt.com/) |
| Show HN (Hacker News) | Things people can try; technical audience | Free | [Show HN rules](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html) |
| AlternativeTo | Any software; "alternative to X" searches | Free | [alternativeto.net](https://alternativeto.net/) |
| Indie Hackers products | Indie products with a story | Free | [indiehackers.com/products](https://www.indiehackers.com/products) |
| Your platform's store | Where your users install things | See Level 3 | — |

|---|---|---|---|

**Not free / gone:** the Chrome Web Store needs a one-time developer fee. The Amazon Appstore for Android devices
closed on 20 Aug 2025 (only Fire devices remain). Many "AI tool directories" charge to submit.

## Steps
1. **Build the launch kit once (2 h).** Fill `assets/launch-kit.md`: name, a 60-character tagline, 160- and
   500-character descriptions, logo (512 px and 240 px), 3–5 screenshots, a 15–30 s demo GIF or video, categories,
   pricing, a founder bio, and links with UTM tags per site (`?utm_source=uneed`).
2. **Pick 10–15 sites that fit (15 min).** All of Level 1, the Level 2 sites that match your audience, and the
   Level 3 stores for your platform. Skip sites where your product obviously doesn't belong.
3. **Submit in waves.** Level 1 in launch week. Level 2 spread over the next 2–4 weeks, so each gives you its own
   bump. Level 3 as soon as your builds are ready for those stores.
4. **Log everything** in `assets/directory-tracker.csv`: site, date, status, live URL, UTM, visits, sign-ups.
5. **Show up.** Reply to comments and reviews on each listing, just as you would on Product Hunt.
6. **Review after 30 days.** Which 3 sites sent *activated* users? Update those listings with each release, and
   ignore the rest.

## Prompt (copy-paste)
Replace everything in `{{ }}`.

````text
You are an indie launch assistant. Help me list my product on free directories and alternative stores.

Product: {{what it does, who for, URL}}
Type + platforms: {{iOS / Android / Mac / Windows / web SaaS / browser extension / game / open source?}}
Pricing: {{free / freemium / paid}}   Audience: {{developers / consumers / businesses / ...}}

1. From these levels, choose the 10-15 best-fit places and explain each in one line:
   Level 1: Product Hunt, Show HN, AlternativeTo, Indie Hackers.
   Level 2: Uneed, TinyLaunch, MicroLaunch, Fazier, Peerlist Launchpad, DevHunt, BetaList, SaaSHub, OpenAlternative.
   Level 3: Samsung Galaxy Store, Huawei AppGallery, Xiaomi GetApps, Aptoide, F-Droid, Microsoft Store,
   Firefox Add-ons, Edge Add-ons, Softpedia, MacUpdate, Setapp, itch.io, awesome lists.
   Skip anything that doesn't fit my platform or audience (e.g. F-Droid only for open-source Android apps).
2. Fill my launch kit: 60-char tagline, 160-char and 500-char descriptions, 5 categories/tags,
   and a UTM link per chosen site.
3. A 4-week submission schedule (Level 1 in launch week, Level 2 spread out, Level 3 when builds are ready).
Remind me to check each site's current pricing. Don't claim a site is free if you're not sure.
````

## Common mistakes
- **Paying for "submit to 100+ directories" services.** Most of those sites send no users, and the backlinks are low quality.
- **A different description on every site, written in a rush.** Write the kit once, then adapt it.
- **No UTM tags,** so after 30 days you can't tell which site worked.
- **Listing the wrong product type,** such as a closed-source app on F-Droid or a consumer app on DevHunt. It gets rejected, or worse, ignored.

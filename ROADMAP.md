## Later (by demand)
New areas open once people ask for them: SEO · content marketing (incl. newsletters) · conversion · retention ·
analytics (beyond the Firebase setup in `launch-growth-stack-setup`). More social: YouTube long-form, influencer outreach. More research skills: user interviews, market sizing. More playbooks: *grow an app*, *monetize a free app*.
Translations (Vietnamese first) are welcome once the English skills are stable.

## Principles
- Depth over breadth. A new area opens only after it has at least 3 good skills.
- Every skill: under an hour, a copy-paste prompt, and a concrete output.
- Numbers are real or labelled *Illustrative*.

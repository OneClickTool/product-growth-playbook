# Daily Instagram flow for an indie dev

Total: **~20 min a day + 2–3 h batching on Sunday + 30 min once a month.** Posts are made in a batch with the
[image flow](../docs/04-image-flow.md); the daily part is publishing, Stories and answering.

## Every day (≈20 min)

| When | Min | Do | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Your slot (post days: Mon, Wed, Thu, Fri, Sun) | 3 | Publish the scheduled post; set bio link 1 to its product ([06](../docs/06-stories-and-links.md)) | 1 post live |
| First 60 min after | 10 | Answer every comment and DM with a real reply | Conversation |
| +1–3 h | 5 | 3-frame Story with link sticker ([template](../templates/story-sequence.md)); add to Highlight | 1 Story sequence |
| Off days (Tue, Sat) | 5 | One Story: behind the scenes, a poll, "what shipped" | Followers keep seeing you |
| Any time | 0 | Shipped / learned something? One line in the fact bank | Material for next week |
| Evening | 2 | 3–5 thoughtful comments on posts from accounts your buyers follow (Practice) | Profile visits from their audience |

## Every Sunday (≈2–3 h)

1. **Numbers (10 min):** fill [templates/weekly-numbers.md](../templates/weekly-numbers.md), find the weakest arrow
   ([07](../docs/07-metrics-and-stop-rules.md)).
2. **Plan (15 min):** pick next week's 5 posts with the rotation in [05](../docs/05-post-formats.md) and one fact
   each (agent: `/social-instagram week`).
3. **Make (90–120 min):** run the [image flow](../docs/04-image-flow.md) for each post: brief → slides → export →
   caption → post file. Record all Reel clips in one session.
4. **Schedule (10 min):** in Instagram's own scheduler or Meta Business Suite; write the Story sequences as files
   next to the posts.

## Once a month (≈30 min)
- Keep the 2 best formats, drop the weakest, add one new one (Trial Reel first).
- Refresh pinned posts and Highlights; check every bio and sticker link still lands on the right page.
- Check **Account Status** for recommendation limits.
- Re-check [reference/instagram-facts.md](../reference/instagram-facts.md) and date any change.

## Daily checklist (copy into your notes)
```text
[ ] post live (post days) + bio link 1 = today's product
[ ] answered every comment / DM in the first hour
[ ] Story sequence with link sticker → Highlight
[ ] 3–5 real comments on buyer-side accounts
[ ] 1 new fact in the fact bank (if any)
```

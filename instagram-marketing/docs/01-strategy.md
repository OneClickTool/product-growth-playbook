# 01 · Strategy: what Instagram is for an indie dev

## The job Instagram does
X is where makers talk to makers. Instagram is where **buyers who aren't makers** scroll: Mac and iPhone users,
students, freelancers, people who want a tidier desktop or a nicer week recap. They don't read threads; they swipe
images and watch 15-second clips. So the unit of work here is **one image post that shows a result**.

```text
Goal per post:    impressions from non-followers  (Explore, Reels, suggested posts, Google)
Goal per week:    profile visits → link taps → landing visits with utm_source=instagram
Goal per month:   trials / emails / first paid users that Instagram can be credited for
```

## Who should spend time here
| Product | Instagram fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| iPhone / Android consumer app with a visual result (recap cards, photos, maps, habits) | **High** | The result *is* an image people share |
| Mac utility with a before/after (messy → clean, 0 → done) | **High** | Before/after carousels get saved and sent |
| Dev tool (CLI, terminal, API) | Low–medium | Buyers are on X / Reddit / GitHub; use Instagram only for screen-recorded Reels |
| Game | High | Reels of play moments; carousels of levels / characters |
| Template / Notion / design asset | High | The product is visual by nature |

If your fit is low, keep Instagram to **one Reel a week** recycled from your X demo clip and spend the rest on X.

## The three loops
1. **Reach loop (strangers):** carousel or Reel that people **send** and **save** → Explore / Reels test it with more
   non-followers. Signals: sends per reach, saves, watching or swiping to the end ([03](03-how-instagram-ranks.md)).
2. **Trust loop (followers):** Stories most days, answer every comment and DM, show what shipped. Followers see
   Stories first; that is where the link sticker lives.
3. **Visit loop (website):** every post gives one reason to open the profile ("full checklist in my bio link",
   "Story today has the link"). Profile → bio link with UTM → landing → trial / email ([06](06-stories-and-links.md)).

## The mix (Practice)
Per week: **3 carousels + 1 Reel + 1 single image**, Stories on 4–5 days, 3 of them with a link sticker.
At most **one post in five** is about the product's price or offer. The rest teach, show and prove.

## What success looks like after 30 days (set your own bar from your baseline)
- Non-follower share of reach above half on carousels and Reels (Insights → post → Reach).
- Sends + saves per 1,000 reach rising week over week.
- Profile visits → link taps → landing visits each non-zero and tracked by UTM.
- One format you keep, one you drop ([07](07-metrics-and-stop-rules.md)).

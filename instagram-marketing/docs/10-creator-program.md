# 10 · Creator program: other people's audiences, on a budget

The fastest consumer-app growth stories on short-form social are creator stories: Cal AI reportedly ran 250+ paid
fitness creators making simple product demos, paid upfront for several videos at once, then reused the best clips
as ads (Reported, founder interviews). Meta reports partnership ads (ads run from a creator's post) average 19 %
lower CPA than brand ads (Reported). This page is how a solo dev runs a small version of that.

## 1 · Who to find (60 min to build the first list of 100)

| Look for | Skip |
|---|---|
| 5k–100k followers in the buyer's niche (Mac setups, iPhone tips, productivity, students, travel journaling, desk tours) | Big accounts with generic "lifestyle" content |
| Reels with **views well above their follower count** (the ranker likes them now) | Accounts with high followers and tiny views (bought or dead) |
| Comments from real people asking "what app is that?" | Comment sections full of emojis from pods |
| Already shows apps / tools on screen | Only faces, no screens (unless your product is filmed in hand) |

Where: Instagram search for your 30 queries ([09, engine 8](09-growth-engine.md#engine-8--search-instagram--google)),
hashtags of the niche, the "suggested accounts" row on a good creator's profile, TikTok creators who also post
Reels, Meta's creator marketplace in Business Suite if your account has it. Track in
[templates/creator-tracker.md](../templates/creator-tracker.md).

## 2 · Outreach (the numbers game)

Expect few replies: Cal AI's founder said roughly 1 in 100 answer (Reported). Plan volume, not perfection:
**60–100 DMs a week** gets a handful of real conversations.

DM 1 (short, specific, no attachment):
```text
Hey {{name}}, your Reel on {{their topic}} is exactly how my users talk about {{problem}}.
I make {{product}} ({{one line}}). Would you try it free? If you like it, I'd pay for {{3}} Reels.
No script, your honest take.
```
Follow-up after 4 days (once): `Quick bump, happy to send a free {{Pro}} code either way.`
Email if the bio has one; it often gets a better answer than DMs (Practice).

## 3 · The deal

| Level | Give | Get | Rights |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gifted | Free Pro / lifetime license, early access | A post **only if** they want, honest | Organic only |
| Paid pack | Fixed fee for 3–5 Reels, paid 50 % upfront | Reels on their account, Collab with you | **Usage rights**: you may run them as partnership ads for 60–90 days |
| Retainer | Monthly fee + bonus per {{100k}} views or per trial via their code | 4–8 Reels a month | Ads rights, raw files |
| Affiliate | % of revenue via their code / link (RevenueCat, Stripe, Lemon Squeezy, Gumroad support codes or links) | Ongoing posts | Organic |

Price: ask their rate first; anchor on their **average views**, not followers. Pay per pack, not per post, to lower
the per-video price (what Cal AI reported). Start with one pack, renew only creators whose posts bring landing visits.

## 4 · The brief (one page, never a script)
Template: [templates/creator-brief.md](../templates/creator-brief.md).
- The **one result** to show in the first 2 seconds (the clean folder, the recap card).
- 3 true facts they may say; the offer and the link / code.
- What not to say: invented numbers, "best app ever" claims they don't believe, health/money promises.
- **Disclosure:** Instagram's **Paid partnership** label on paid or gifted posts, plus `#ad` where local rules want
  it. The 2023 FTC Endorsement Guides require clear disclosure of payment or free product (Official, US).
- Collab invite to your brand account; raw file sent to you for ads.

## 5 · Track and scale
- One UTM per creator: `utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=creator&utm_campaign=<product>&utm_content=<handle>` and/or a
  promo code.
- At +7 days: views, shares, profile visits to you (Insights on the collab), landing visits, trials.
- **Keep the top 20 %**, put their best Reel into partnership ads ([09, engine 7](09-growth-engine.md#engine-7--paid-amplifier-money-only-on-proven-winners)), give them a retainer.
- Ask winners for **3 new hooks on the same video**: run them as Trial Reels on their account or as ad variants.

## 6 · Never
- Ask for a fake review, fake "I found this app" story, or a hidden paid post.
- Buy "shoutouts" from accounts with fake followers.
- Run creator content as ads without written permission.

## Rules
- Hook ≤ 8 words on screen; the image or first frame shows the same thing.
- Never a hook the post doesn't deliver. Misleading hooks lose the swipe, and the swipe is the signal.
- No "Ever wondered", "You won't believe", "🚨", "This changes everything".

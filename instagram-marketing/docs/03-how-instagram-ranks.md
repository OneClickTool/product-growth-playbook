# 03 · How Instagram ranks posts, in plain words

Sources and dates for every line: [reference/instagram-facts.md](../reference/instagram-facts.md).

## There are four rankers, not one (Official)

| Surface | Who sees it | What it predicts | What to make |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Feed** | Mostly followers (+ suggested posts) | Time spent, comment, like, share, tap on your photo | Carousels people swipe through; a question at the end |
| **Stories** | Followers who watch you | Tap in, reply, move to the next | Short sequences, a poll or question sticker, the link sticker |
| **Explore** | Mostly non-followers | Likes, **saves**, **shares**; popularity counts more here | Saveable carousels: checklists, steps, before/after |
| **Reels** | Mostly non-followers | **Reshare**, **watch to the end**, like, audio-page visit | 7–30 s screen recordings with the result in the first second |

## The three signals Mosseri named (Reported, Jan 2025)
**Watch time, likes and sends.** Read them **per reach**, not as totals. Likes matter a bit more with followers;
**sends** (sharing by DM) matter a bit more with non-followers. For growth, design every post to be sent:

- *"Send this to the friend whose Downloads folder is a mess."* is a post someone can act on.
- A checklist, a before/after, a "3 settings you never changed" are things people forward.

## What gets shown less (Official)
- Reels with low resolution, a **watermark** from another app, muted audio, borders, **mostly text**, or that were
  already posted on Instagram.
- Content that breaks the Recommendation Guidelines: it stays up but isn't recommended. Repeat it and the whole
  account stops being recommended for a while (check Account Status).
- Since Apr 30, 2026 (Reported): accounts whose posts are mostly **other people's work** aren't recommended, now for
  photos and carousels too, judged over about a month. Credit lines don't fix it. Your own screenshots, your own
  numbers, your own words do.

## What that means for an indie dev
1. **Export clean.** Record Reels in the app or with your own screen recorder, not downloaded from TikTok with its
   logo. Re-export your X clips without overlays.
2. **Don't make "mostly text" Reels.** Text-heavy ideas go in a carousel, where swiping is the point. A Reel needs
   motion: the app doing the thing.
3. **The first slide / first second decides.** If the hook doesn't stop the scroll, nothing else is seen.
4. **Ask for the send, once, plainly**, on the last slide. Never "comment X to get Y" as bait for likes (see
   [08](08-rules-and-risks.md)); a keyword-to-DM link is fine when the DM really contains the link.
5. **Captions are search text.** Instagram search and Google read them: name the problem the way a buyer types it
   ("find duplicate files on Mac"), then hashtags (≤5, Reported).
6. **Trial Reels** let you test a Reel on non-followers first (Reported). Use them for every new format, so a flop
   doesn't land in your followers' feed.

## What nobody outside Instagram knows
Exact weights, a "best hour", whether a link in bio "hurts reach". Treat every such claim as Practice and test it
against your own Insights.

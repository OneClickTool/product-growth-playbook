## Brief · Sweepy

**Show in the first 2 s:** a Downloads folder full of `report (1).pdf, report (2).pdf…`, then the scan result.
**True facts:** finds duplicates byte for byte, even across folders · flags old .dmg/.pkg (7+ days), 90-day-unopened
and 500 MB+ files, never pre-selected · moves to Trash, never deletes; undo 7 days (Free) / 30 days (Pro) · sorts
loose files by type and month · Free watches 2 folders.
**Offer:** Free for 2 folders · Pro $9.99 once (US), in-app. **Link:**
`https://oneclicktool.app/macos/sweepy?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=creator&utm_campaign=sweepy&utm_content=<handle>`
**Don't say:** "frees X GB" unless you measured it on your Mac; "deletes" (it moves to Trash).

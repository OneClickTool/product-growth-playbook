# 30-day Instagram plan · Oct 12 → Nov 10, 2026

> Disclosure: the products are the maintainer's (OneClickTool). Formats: [docs/05](../docs/05-post-formats.md) ·
> scripts: [product-rotation.md](product-rotation.md) · each post via the [image flow](../docs/04-image-flow.md).
> No targets from blogs: every check compares with **your own week 1**.

## Week by week

| Week | Goal | Posts (Mon · Wed · Thu · Fri · Sun) | Stories with link | Check on Sunday |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** Oct 12–18 | Profile live, baseline | [week 1](week-01-starter.md): start-here · Edge Tabs Reel (trial) · Sweepy I05 · Haunts I10 · Markdown I06 | Wed, Fri, Sat | Baseline numbers; every link tapped on a phone |
| **2** Oct 19–25 | Find the send-able format | Sweepy I03 checklist · AgentBell Reel (trial) · Sweepy I02 before/after · Haunts I08 privacy · Edge Tabs I04 tips | Mon, Wed, Sat | Sends + saves per 1k reach per format |
| **3** Oct 26–Nov 1 | Turn reach into profile visits | Haunts I11 poll carousel · Sweepy Reel (trial) · Redliner I08 building · Markdown Reel · Sweepy I12 offer (the one offer) | Mon, Wed, Sun | Profile visits per post; is the last slide naming the path? |
| **4** Nov 2–8 | Turn profile visits into site visits | Repeat week 2's best format on a new product · its Reel version · best carousel → X post · Haunts recap card #2 · numbers I09 if any are real | Mon, Wed, Fri, Sat | Link taps → landing visits by `utm_content` |
| Nov 9–10 | Decide | Keep 2 formats, drop 1, try 1; refresh pins and Highlights | — | Apply the [stop rules](../docs/07-metrics-and-stop-rules.md) |

## Every day (20 min)
Publish (post days) · bio link 1 = today's product · answer everything in the first hour · Story at +1–3 h with the
sticker and save it to the Highlight · 3–5 real comments on accounts your buyers follow
([flow/daily-flow.md](../flow/daily-flow.md)).

## Every Sunday (2–3 h)
Numbers (10 min) → plan next week (15 min, `/social-instagram week`) → build 5 posts (90–120 min) → schedule (10 min).

## Stop signs (act the same day)
- Account Status shows a recommendation limit → stop posting new formats, read [08](../docs/08-rules-and-risks.md), fix, appeal if wrong.
- A post's link lands on a 404 or the wrong product → fix bio link and Highlight before anything else.
- You're out of real facts for a product → skip it this week; don't fill slides with claims you can't back.

## After day 30
- Instagram sends < 5 % of landing visits and no trials → keep 1 Reel a week recycled from X, move the hours to X.
- Otherwise → keep the rotation, add one Trial Reel a week, and turn the best carousel of the month into an
  [X long post](../../x-marketing/docs/09-post-variants.md).

# Research log · 2026-10-12 · Instagram for indie devs

**Question:** what does Instagram itself say about reach, and what is the shortest honest path from an image post
to a website visit?

## What was checked
- Instagram's own ranking explainer (fetched, dated May 31, 2023) → per-surface signals, the Reels "shown less"
  list, Recommendation Guidelines, Account Status.
- Mosseri's Jan 2025 statements on watch time / likes / sends (press coverage; original video not fetched).
- Press on: 3:4 photos (May 2025), taller grid and 3-minute Reels (Jan 2025), Trial Reels (Dec 2024), Google
  indexing of professional accounts (Jul 2025), 5-hashtag cap (Dec 2025), caption-link test for Meta Verified
  (Mar 2026), the originality rule for photos and carousels (Apr 30, 2026).
- Instagram Help / blog: website in bio, link sticker for everyone, `#`/`@` links in bio, alt text.

## What it means
1. Reach to strangers comes from Explore and Reels, both of which reward **sends, saves and watching to the end**.
   For a dev with no face on camera, the carousel (swipe = time spent) and the screen-recorded Reel are the two
   formats that fit.
2. Since Apr 2026 originality is judged across photos and carousels too, so "tips" pages built from other people's
   screenshots won't be recommended. Show your own product and your own numbers.
3. A caption URL does nothing. The visit happens through the bio links, the Story link sticker (24 h, keep it in a
   Highlight), or a DM. Every post therefore needs a reason to tap the profile.
4. Posts from a professional account can show in Google: write captions with the words a buyer searches.
